## Decisions & Context

> [!info]- Background
> The 13-trucks/day target surfaced measurement gaps more than capacity gaps: the team lacks KPIs for approval-to-shipment timing, schedule adherence, and expedited-shipment frequency. Customer Service's working expectation is material approved ~3 business days before shipment. Two named workstreams — the Kaizen event and the 5S/physical-capacity effort — are tracked as their own projects (see Related) because they have their own note trails, but they're sub-efforts of this same ramp-up, not separate initiatives.
