## Decisions & Context

> [!info]- Background
> Japan and Korea affiliates can already receive/process US pallets, removing a repalletization step once the TCA lane opens (~Sep–Oct) and US plastic pallets arrive. Per Brandon Bowler, all IDAP shipments are moving to plastic. Open question still being tracked: whether/how to phase out active containers, especially for low-volume green-tray shipments to Japan.
