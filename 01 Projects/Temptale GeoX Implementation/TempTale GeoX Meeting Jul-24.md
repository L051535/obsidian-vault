**Meeting objective:** Accelerate deployment of TempTale Geo X (GOX) tracking devices following a recent Brazil shipment theft and establish a compliant process for broader GPS tracking across shipments.

### What was decided

- The team agreed to use a **temporary protocol** for rapid implementation rather than waiting for SOP revisions.
- Separate protocols will be created for **NALO** and **RTP** due to different site ownership and approval requirements.
- GOX deployment is expected to expand significantly, potentially becoming a standard requirement across shipments.
- RTP and NALO will each manage their own device inventory and execution processes.

### Key discussion points

- A stolen shipment in Brazil heightened the need for stronger shipment visibility and real-time monitoring.
- SOP updates are still required, but protocols were viewed as the fastest way to implement the change while formal change controls proceed.
- Device availability is a concern. Additional GOX units are being requested from Sensitech and expedited delivery is being explored.
- The group discussed how GOX devices will be applied across shipment types, including active containers, TCA shipments, and ocean shipments.
- Participants emphasized the need for documented training, clear ownership, and auditable processes before relying on external logistics providers to place devices.

### Risks & Concerns

- Current device inventory may not be sufficient if deployment expands rapidly.
- Most Brazil shipments are currently shipping from RTP, increasing urgency to establish RTP processes quickly.
- Relying on freight forwarders without documented procedures and training could create compliance and accountability gaps.

### Action Items

- **Khalaff & Helen:** Draft and route NALO and RTP protocols.
- **Khalaff:** Confirm device availability and expedited supply with Sensitech.
- **Khalaff:** Continue SOP/change-control activities for permanent implementation.
- **Khalaff:** Coordinate training plans and RTP rollout activities.
- **Review Team:** Prioritize protocol review and approval to enable immediate deployment.

### Bottom Line

The meeting was driven by a security-related response to the Brazil theft event. The team aligned on a fast-track approach: deploy GOX devices through site-specific protocols now, secure additional inventory, train personnel, and later incorporate the process into formal SOPs. The primary focus is balancing speed of implementation with documentation, training, and quality compliance requirements.
