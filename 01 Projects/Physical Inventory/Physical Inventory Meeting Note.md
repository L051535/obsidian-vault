### Meeting Summary: PDC Full Physical Inventory LT/Process Lead Alignment

**Meeting Objective**  
The meeting focused on planning NALO PDC's first full physical inventory, aligning leadership and process leads on the execution strategy, resource requirements, risks, training, and operational impacts. The team reviewed a proposed inventory shutdown plan, discussed alternatives to reduce downtime, and identified support needed from Operations, QA, IT, Customer Service, and other functions. Event

**Note:** Information below is summarized and paraphrased from the meeting transcript. The meeing was transcribed. Event

### Key Decisions & Direction

#### 1. Physical Inventory Approach

- A full warehouse inventor of approximately **5,100 locations** is planned.
- Initial proposals called for a **3-day operational shutdown**, but the presenter shared data suggesting the count may be completed in a shorter timeframe.
- Inventory movements, receipts, and PGIs would be stopped during counting.
- Discrepancies would be documented and investigated after counting rather than delaying inventory completion. One deviation is planned to capture all inventory adjustments rather than a separate deviation for each discrepancy. Event

#### 2. Counting Strategy

- Historical cycle count data showed high counting rates with low dicrepancy levels, giving confidence that the inventory can be completed within the proposed window.
- Teams discussed moving partial pallets to A-level locations before the event to simplify counting and increase efficiency.
- Full pallets are expected to count significantly faster than partial pallets.
- The team reviewed milestone targets by day, including completion goals for export, CRT, chill, and forward-pick locations. Event

#### 3. Training & Documentation

- The entire execution process will be documented within a prtocol.
- Personnel involved will be trained before execution.
- Training is expected to be managed through protocol-based documentation due to timeline constraints.
- Leadership emphasized that all procedures, responsibilities, headcount needs, and escalation paths must be documented before execution. Event

### Risk Discussion

Several leaders expressed concern that:

- This is the site's first full hysical inventory.
- The proposed execution timelines are based on theoretical performance and previous cycle count experience.
- Reducing shutdown duration introduces risk if unexpected issues arise.
- Buffer time may be needed before resuming operations and customer shipments.
- The facility should avoid compromising inventory integrity by releasing areas prematurely or allowing material movement before verification is complete. Event

The team debated:

- Whether portions of the warehouse could be pre-counted and segregated befoe the event.
- Whether inventory volumes could be reduced in advance through off-site storage or ASRS utilization.
- Whether shipping activity on Sunday would create unnecessary risk during inventory execution. Event

### Operational Preparation Activities

The team identified several key pre-work activities:

Minimize inventory levels where possible.

- Consolidate and reduce partial pallets.
- Move qualifying inventory into ASRS storage.
- Move partial pallets to A-level locations.
- Finalize organizational structure and staffing assignments.
- Complete protocol approval and effectiveness activities.
- Prepare count tracking tools, placards, and status reporting mechanisms.
- Establish a progress-tracking process using EWM data and visual status reporting. Event

### Resource Requirements

#### Team Structure

- Five counting teams are planned.
- Each team s expected to have:
    - Dedicated counters
    - Recount resources
    - Team leaders responsible for status reporting and escalation

#### Cross-Functional Support Requested

The inventory effort requires support beyond Operations, including:

- QA
- IT
- Customer Service
- Transportation
- Other support functions

The team requested approximately:

- **10 team leaders per day** across Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
- Additional QA support for issue resolution and inventory-related decisions.
- IT support availability throughout the event, especially for system access and warehouse technology issues. Event

### Inventory Accuracy & Future State Discussion

A significant portion of the meeting focusedon the broader inventory management strategy.

Key themes included:

- The physical inventory is intended to establish confidence in inventory accuracy.
- The group expects discrepancies to be identified.
- Corrective and preventive actions (CAPAs) will need to be developed after inventory completion.
- Leaders emphasized that cycle count processes may need strengthening to prevent future inventory accuracy issues.
- Participants discussed how mature inventory management programs rely heavily on ongoing cycle counting to avoid repeated full physical inventories. Event

### Open Items / Action Items

1. Complete and approve the inventory protocol.
2. Finalize prework checklist/Gantt-style planning activities.
3. Determine inventory reduction opportunities before October execution.
4. Identify all team leaders and support personnel.
5. Provide names of cross-functional resources by approximately **September 25**.
6. Confirm QA, IT, Customer Service, and Transportation support models.
7. Define post-inventory discrepancy investigation and CAPA process.
8. Address warehouse access requirements for non-operations personnel serving as team leaders. Event

### Overall Outcome

Leadership generally supported continuing detailed planning for the full hysical inventory but emphasized the need to:

- Manage operational risk carefully.
- Ensure comprehensive documentation and training.
- Build sufficient contingency plans.
- Clearly define cross-functional support needs.
- Use the event not only to verify inventory but also to improve the site's long-term inventory control and cycle counting processes.
