# NALO Site Self-Inspection and Internal Auditing Program

**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Quality Assurance, and NALO support areas
**Associated Documents:** Attachment 1 (NALO Site Self-Inspection Risk Assessment), Attachment 4 (NALO Site Self-Inspection topics), Attachment 5 (North American Logistics Operations Site Self-Inspection Report Template)

## Purpose

This procedure provides details of activities, roles, and responsibilities to address the requirements in LQS111, Quality Auditing. The NALO Site Self-Inspection and Internal Auditing Program is designed to ensure the adequacy and effectiveness of NALO quality systems and ensure compliance to regulations, CGMPs, and all governing Lilly Standards. Self-inspections and auditing are proactive means of improving quality.

This procedure describes:
- The process for creating, maintaining, and closing an annual Site Self-Inspection plan and the process for conducting and documenting Site Self-Inspections.
- The Internal Auditing Program executed by GQAAC (Global Quality Auditing and Compliance) and PDS (Pharmaceutical Delivery Systems) at NALO.

## Overview

The NALO Site Self-Inspection (SSI) is a risk-based program executed annually by NALO personnel. The SSI Coordinator is responsible for creating the annual NALO SSI Plan, which must be approved annually by the Site Quality Leader no later than the end of the first quarter of each calendar year.

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| SSI Coordinator | Creates the annual SSI Risk Plan and Schedule in the quality management electronic system. |
| NALO Quality Lead Team | Reviews the annual SSI Risk Plan and Self Inspection Schedule. |
| NALO Quality Leader | Approves critical observations and Response Reports. Approves all requests for distribution of Inspection Reports outside of Lilly as well as requests from regulatory authorities. |
| NALO Quality Inspection Area Manager/Supervisor | Approves the Self Inspection Plan and Schedule. Designates Lead Inspectors after completion of Curriculum ID: C0111791 (DOAD160: NALO QA Lead Inspector). |
| Lead Inspector | May serve as a Lead Inspector; must not approve their own records. Approves inspection plan changes, Inspection Reports, and Response Reports. Provides resources such as personnel to participate in the inspection. Approves Inspection Reports. Submits Inspection Response Reports for approval via Veeva. Submits Critical Observation Response Reports within 20 business days of Inspection Report approval. Completes Curriculum ID: C0111791. |
| Inspector | Conducts the Self Inspection of the area, submits the Inspection Report for approval. Notifies Inspection Area Manager of significant findings. Notifies Inspection Area Manager and Site Quality Leader if a potential Critical Inspection Observation is identified. Assists the Lead Inspector with all SSI activities. |

## Lead Inspector Training Requirements

To be qualified as a Lead Inspector, personnel are required to complete Curriculum ID: C0111791 (DOAD160: NALO QA Lead Inspector) and have experience participating in a past SSI or audit.

## SSI Risk Plan and Schedule

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Create the SSI Risk Plan using Attachment 1. The scope and system/area to inspect are based on the overall identified level of risk; the time frame for SSI activity execution is established on a quarterly basis. |
| 2 | Present Attachment 1 during the NALO Quality Lead Team (QLT) meeting to gain agreement to proceed and upload to Veeva for final approval. |
| 3 | Create a quality management electronic record to schedule and track execution of the SSI activities in alignment with the approved SSI Risk Plan. Assign each SSI work item a Lead Inspector. |

> [!note]
> Approval signatures indicate that the identified areas of focus for SSI are aligned with the overall risk plan and timing of execution is appropriate.

## Execution

When preparing to execute an SSI activity (Lead Inspector, Inspector, and NALO support areas responsible), consider:
- Define the scope using Attachment 4.
- Identify any changes related to the area to be inspected.
- Read the SOPs and documents associated with the area to be inspected — ideally prepare a list of questions to ensure all areas of the processes are covered.
- Review actions from previous inspections.
- Secure timing with the area manager/supervisor and personnel to hold an opening meeting, execute the inspection, and hold a closing meeting.

For all planned SSI activities: execute within the timeframe documented in the SSI plan, network with Business area SMEs, and ensure procedures/processes/systems include data integrity by design aligned with GQS105.

For all completing SSI activities (Lead Inspector responsible): document completion using Attachment 5, upload to Veeva, and send to a NALO QA representative (R4/P4/M2 level or above) for approval within 30 days.

## SSI Observations Classification

SSI observations are classified as minor, major, or critical according to the risk to product quality and data integrity, per Appendix 1 – Audit Observations Classification in LQS111.

> [!warning]
> Critical observations must be communicated via Notification to Management per GSOP-101-3.

## SSI Responses

| Response | Expectation | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Response Submission | Area process owner submits responses within 15 days of the observation summary completion. Download the approved SSI Observation from Veeva and complete the Response section. | NALO Area SME as assigned by Lead Inspector |
| Response Approval of Non-Critical Observations | Each response must include: elements of CAPA (corrective/preventative actions, with rationale if no action taken), an implementation timeline, and name of the implementation owner. | Functional Management and NALO Quality Management |
| Response Approval to Critical Observations | Responses must be developed, added to the report, and approved within 30 days of receipt of the written observation(s). Approval signature by the Lead Inspector indicates each response addresses root causes appropriately. Approval by NALO Area Management and NALO Quality Management indicates actions and timing of implementation are appropriate. | Lead Inspector, NALO Operations Management, NALO Quality Management, and the Global Quality Leader |

> [!note]
> The response to a critical observation must be developed and approved within 20 business days of receipt of the written observation (date of Lead Inspector signature on the inspection report). Critical Observation Response Reports must be approved within 5 business days of submission. All other Response Reports must be approved within 60 business days of submission.

## Access to SSI Report

SSI reports are internal, confidential documents with controlled distributions and are stored in a secure EDMS repository to prevent unauthorized access. SSI reports must not be distributed outside Lilly or provided to regulatory authorities without approval of the Global Quality Leader. Refer to LQS112.

## Internal Auditing Program

**Overview:** NALO is an auditable unit included in the approved annual risk-based audit plan developed respectively by Global Quality Auditing and Compliance (GQAAC) and Pharmaceutical Delivery Systems (PDS).

GQAAC and PDS Risk assessments are conducted annually to define the frequency and scope of internal audit considering factors such as: overall compliance status and history, robustness of quality risk management activities, data integrity, complexity of the site, results of previous audits/inspections, and major changes to building/equipment/processes.

Audits risk assessment, planning, execution, reporting and responses are performed in accordance with GQAAC-SOP003-GQAAC Auditing Program for internal audits executed by GQAAC and PDS-SOP-PDS4152 for internal audits executed by Indianapolis Device Manufacturing (IDM) for PDS.

**Audit Types:**
- **Routine/Surveillance Audits** – Planned audits to verify the appropriateness and effectiveness of the implementation and operation of the system and/or processes.
- **For Cause/Directed Audits** – Performed for any potential quality related issues that may impact product quality, patient safety, or data integrity or marketing authorization.
- **Quality Due Diligence Assessments (QDDA)** – High level risk-based review of potential suppliers and service providers, led by GQAAC.

## Audit Criteria

The following audit criteria will be utilized, as applicable (additional standards/regulations not explicitly listed may also be used):

21 CFR Parts 3, 4, 11, 210/211, 801, 803, 806, and 820; ISO 13485:2016; ISO 14971:2019; Council Directive 93/42/EEC MDD and EU Regulation 2017/745 MDR; Council Directive 2014/53/EU Radio Equipment Directive (EU RED); Statutory Instrument 2017 No. 1206, UK Radio Equipment Regulations 2017 (UK RED); Statutory Instrument 2002 No. 618, UK MDR 2002; MDSAP AU P0002; CMDR SOR/98-282; Japan MHLW Ordinance 145 and 169; Brazil RDC ANVISA 665/2022; Australia Therapeutic Goods (Medical Devices) Act; Eli Lilly and Company Global Quality Standards (GQS); Eli Lilly and Company Quality Standards (LQS).

## Access to Internal Audit Report

Audit reports are internal, confidential documents with controlled distributions. Audit reports must not be distributed outside Lilly or provided to regulatory authorities without approval of the Global Quality Leader. Refer to LQS112.

## Record Retention

Records of NALO SSI and internal audits performed by GQAAC and IDM for PDS are maintained in accordance with the requirements of the Global Records Retention Schedule.
