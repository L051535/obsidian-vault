# Development and Revision of Site Quality Plans for North American Logistics Operations

**Supersedes:** 001-004532
**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Quality Assurance, and NALO support areas

## Purpose

To describe the minimum requirements for development, execution, and monitoring process for North American Logistics Operations (NALO) Site Quality Plan.

## References

- Global Records Retention Schedule
- GQS 101, Quality Management
- LQS 101, Quality Management
- USD-SOP-Activity Planning TW1000-170

## Reasons for Revision

No revisions were necessary at periodic review.

## Introduction

The purpose of the NALO Site Quality Plan is a critical tool for documenting the quality actions or initiatives that will be completed to close compliance gaps, meet global commitments, and/or improve practices to keep NALO current with industry and regulatory expectations.

Quality initiatives and critical improvements presented in the plan could derive from, but are not limited to:

- Required elements from the Global Quality Plan
- Internal Audits/Self-Inspection, External audits, and regulatory inspections
- Management Review Reports
- Site Deviation quarterly trend reports
- Significant actions identified as a result of the site's CAPA program
- New Lilly Quality Standards (LQS) and new/revised Global Quality Standards (GQS)
- Business strategies and Site strategic objectives
- Industry Trends
- New technologies or technology advances

NALO Site Quality Plans actions are created in the TrackWise Activity Planning module according to USD-SOP-Activity Planning TW1000-170. Quality Plan actions are designed by a field in TrackWise (in a Work Item or Work Action). Records can be created individually or as part of a Work Plan, depending on the source of the action.

## Definition

**Site Quality Plan** – A written plan, consistent with global and site strategy, used to identify the activities necessary to address quality initiatives and issues, and monitor implementation and results.

## Roles and Responsibilities for Site Quality Plan Requirements

1. **Site Quality Plan Steward (Site Quality Assurance Compliance)** will:
   - Annually initiate a new Quality Plan in TrackWise. Any open actions from the previous year may be transferred to the current year's plan, justification provided for closing the action, or continuing to monitor the action to closure.
   - Utilize the listed resources from the Introduction and work with departments to determine which items should be placed on the plan.
   - Assign the responsible person, where possible, for the completion action in TrackWise.
   - Ensure that the NALO Site Quality Plan is monitored regularly and is modified based on input from each NALO department.
2. **Project Owner** will:
   - Be assigned as the Action Owner in TrackWise.
   - Ensure timely completion of projects and associated milestones.
   - Document project completion in TrackWise assigned action and attach objective evidence (if applicable).
   - Present proposal for due date changes to NALO Quality Lead Team and submit request for due date changes for action in TrackWise that cannot be completed by due date as needed.
3. **The Quality Lead Team** will:
   - Ensure that at least annually, the activities contained in the NALO Site Quality Plan are identified, reviewed, and assigned appropriate resources.
   - Review changes to the NALO Site Quality Plan as needed.
4. **The NALO Site head, NALO Site Quality Leader, and Global Quality Leader** will:
   - Approve the annual NALO Site Quality Plan which includes action plans with target due dates.
   - Approve all changes to the NALO Site Quality Plan.

   > [!note]
   > A change in Target Due Date requires an assessment of the impact of the date change to compliance to be noted in the Justification for Date Change field.

5. **NALO Site Quality Leader (NALO Quality Management)** will:
   - Report status of the NALO Site Quality Plan periodically to the Global Quality Leader (Senior Quality Management).
   - Escalate unresolved funding and support issues to appropriate level of quality or operational management for resolution.
   - Ensure that NALO Site Quality Plan items are reviewed regularly during the NALO Quality Lead Team meeting so that any resource issues can be identified before the Quality Plan item due date.

## Prioritization of Site Quality Plan Items

The NALO Quality Lead Team prioritizes GMP issues, initiatives, and improvements based on a risk-based prioritization process considering potential to impact product quality, severity of impact, and frequency of occurrence.

## Record Retention

All documentation is retained in accordance with the Global Records Retention Schedule.
