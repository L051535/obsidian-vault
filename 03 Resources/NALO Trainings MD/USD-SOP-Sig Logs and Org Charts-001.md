# Area Signature Logs and Organizational Charts

**Supersedes:** 001-003997
**Areas Involved:** North American Logistics Operations (NALO), NALO Quality Assurance, NALO support areas

## Purpose

To provide instructions for maintaining and storing area signature logs which are used for verification of identity on GMP documents, and organizational charts which indicate the reporting relationships between all levels of personnel within an area.

## References

- Global Records Retention Schedule
- Global Quality Standard 101, Quality Management
- Lilly Quality Standard 105, Data Integrity and Documentation Practices
- DO23676, NALO Good Documentation Practices and Second Person Verification Training

## Reasons for Revision

To update Step 3 of the Contents of Organizational Charts section. Rationale: To reflect the organization chart is updated quarterly.

## Introduction

Management is responsible for ensuring this procedure is implemented for their area. Management initiates a new Area Signature Log for the upcoming year at the beginning of each calendar year (i.e., in January).

The signature log is intended to be used for verification of the person who performs a specific GMP operation or documentation. Therefore, all entries of signatures or initials on GMP documentation must conform to the Area Signature Log. The Area Signature Log is signed in black or blue indelible ink.

Any individual who is involved, directly or indirectly, in any aspect of GMP operations must sign the Area Signature Log.

If errors are made, follow requirements of DO23676, NALO Good Documentation Practices and Second Person Verification Training.

An organizational chart must also be available for NALO Operations and NALO Quality Assurance. Upon approval of revisions of the Organization Chart, Management ensures copies of the chart are given to Safety Representative(s) for updating the Rally Point Supplies duffel bags.

> [!note]
> The Area Signature Log (USD-SOP-Sig Logs and Org Charts-001 Attachment A) resides within the Electronic Document Management system.

## Signature Logs

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Management ensures that each of his/her employees signs the Area Signature Log within his/her first week in the area or prior to signing GMP documentation. This process is repeated each calendar year at the beginning of the year. |
| 2 | Retain Area Signature Logs in accordance with the Global Records Retention Schedule in the GMP Library in Plainfield or in the GMP Files in Enfield and Fresno. |

> [!note]
> All employees sign the Area Signature Log when the employee transfers into the area. Individual's initials should not be changed unless there is a legal name change or a duplicate of initials is discovered after entry on the log is made. Attachment A is a controlled form and must have a unique identifier either through the Electronic Document Management system or a manual process.

## Contents of Organizational Charts

The organizational chart must depict the reporting structure within the area. The area's organizational chart may be maintained as a stand-alone document. The chart must indicate the management reporting structure up through senior management for NALO (e.g., Sr. Director). This may be accomplished by listing names/titles of management and names of employees by work group or function. The organizational chart must also include the Senior Director, QA IDM as a dotted line report from Associate Director, NALO QA.

The chart will include an effective date. See NALO Organizational Chart Attachment B for an example.

## Procedure for Organizational Charts

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Create the organizational chart to depict reporting relationships. |
| 2 | Obtain approval from area management, or higher level, in QualityDocs. |
| 3 | Update the organization chart once quarterly. |
| 4 | Retain approved and superseded charts in QualityDocs in accordance with the Global Records Retention Schedule. |
