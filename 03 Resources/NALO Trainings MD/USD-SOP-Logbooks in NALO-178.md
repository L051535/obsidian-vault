# Assignment, Use and Review of Logbooks in NALO

**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Tech@Lilly, NALO Engineering and Maintenance, North American Shared Service Center (NASSC-CS), Transportation and Logistics, NALO Quality Assurance and support areas

## Purpose

To describe general requirements for assigning, using, and reviewing logbooks that track forms tied to NALO procedures, ensuring data integrity of GMP-documenting forms (including GMP On-the-Job Training/OJT documents). Barcoding/logbook tracking serves as a business continuity plan if Controlled Print or QualityDocs can't provide forms with unique identifiers.

## Introduction / Definitions

All GMP forms and OJTs not printed via QualityDocs' Controlled Print feature must have a barcode issued before use.

Attachments (in the electronic document management system): **Attachment B** (Master Logbook), **Attachment C** (Form Tracking Data Logbook), **Attachment E** (Controlled GMP Forms).

- **Logbook** — a book of logsheets and/or logsheets and forms, bound by Lilly Document Services or a third-party printer, used to track forms issued from NALO procedures.
- **Master Logbook** — a bound logbook containing Attachment B's data; completed by NALO QA and filed in the NALO GMP Library per the Global Records Retention Schedule. *Does not require a quarterly QA review.*
- **Form** — documentation recording activity execution and/or data collection. Must be controlled if it records raw GMP data, directly indicates a GMP activity occurred, or documents a product-quality decision (e.g., approve/reject).
- **Control** — means: unique identification, controlled access, secure storage, a reconciliation process, and prevention of reproduction.
- **Issuance** — the form/logbook has been printed and/or checked by QA (or designee), recorded in the appropriate logbook, and is ready for use.
- **QA (or designee)** — may be an Operations member trained by QA for logbook assignment/use/review.

## Use, Maintenance, and Documentation Requirements

Logbooks are owned/maintained by QA (or designee) and the areas using them. Owners/users notify QA of new logbooks needed; QA uses the Master Logbook when creating new ones (filed in the Enfield or Plainfield GMP Library as appropriate).

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Write legibly in permanent black or blue ink only. |
| 2 | All entries in chronological order; pages numbered. |
| 3 | Use Good Documentation Practices per DO23676_LL (NALO GDP Leader Led Training). |
| 4 | Use each logbook until retired, full, damaged, or revised — then issue a new one and store the old in the NALO GMP Library. *Give full/damaged/revised logbooks to QA.* |

## QA Maintains Master Logbook

NALO QA completes the Master Logbook whenever a new logbook is established, recording (per Attachment B): logbook number, date assigned/initials, area of use, logbook description/type, logbook barcode, and date retired/initials.

## QA Assigns Logbook Numbers

Format: **SSS-YYYY-DDD-XXX**, where SSS = site of issuance (PDC, EDC), YYYY = year, DDD = Julian date of issuance, XXX = sequential number issued that day. *Example: PDC-2020-234-001.*

## QA Records Information in the Front of Each Logbook

Logbook number, date assigned, area of use, logbook description, barcode, and date retired/initials.

## QA Issues Logbooks

1. Record issuance in the Master Logbook.
2. Apply barcode to the issued logbook and the Master Logbook.
3. Initial/date the issuance in the Master Logbook.
4. Give the logbook to the area to execute.

## Issuance and Use of Forms

QA and executing areas follow these steps. *All barcoded documents must be returned to QA (or designee) for reconciliation — never discarded.*

**For GMP OJTs and forms listed in Attachment E (Controlled GMP Forms):**

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Print the needed form(s) and bring to QA (or designee) for issuance, or request the form from QA. |
| 2 | QA (or designee) issues the form: verify it was printed on the current day and/or is the current effective version; verify all pages are present/numbered correctly; enter the form name in the logbook; apply a barcode to page 1 and the corresponding logbook line; for multi-page forms, write the barcode number and initial/date on each additional GMP-data page; initial/date the issuance in the logbook; give the form to the area to execute. |
| 3 | When complete, the area returns the form to QA (or designee) for reconciliation: confirm all pages are present/complete, and complete the "Form Reconciled Initial/Date" column of Attachment C (Form Tracking Data Logbook) in the appropriate logbook. |
| 4 | File the form per its corresponding procedure or OJT. |

## Review of Logbooks

QA reviews GMP logbooks to confirm content is complete, accurate, and GDP-compliant.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Review logbooks at least quarterly. *Document/investigate any issue found that could impact product quality, per USD-SOP-Deviation Mgmt-001.* |
| 2 | Document "QA review" on the last completed form reviewed, with date and signature/initials. *The logbook stays in use until retired/full/damaged/revised. Each quarterly signature covers the period since the previous signature.* |

## Retiring Logbooks

Logbooks are retired when a user turns in a completed or damaged logbook, or when the revision process determines an active logbook must be replaced.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Before sending for retirement: ensure every page is complete (add "N/A"/"End of Data" or equivalent as appropriate, ensure all necessary signatures are present, handle omissions appropriately). Notify QA when a completed/damaged/replaced logbook needs retiring. |

## Record Retention

Retain per the Global Records Retention Schedule in the NALO GMP Library: the Logbook, the Master Logbook, and Forms Not Bound in Logbooks.
