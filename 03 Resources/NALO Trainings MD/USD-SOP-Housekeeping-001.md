# Housekeeping

**Supersedes:** 001-001389, 022346
**Areas Involved:** North American Logistics Operations (NALO), NALO Engineering and Maintenance
**Areas Affected:** Cleaning service contracted by CBRE.

## Purpose

To ensure that regular housekeeping is performed to provide clean, neat, sanitary warehouses and facilities so that quality of products is not impacted.

## Introduction

Housekeeping is the responsibility of Management of NALO and ensures that the building is maintained according to current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP).

> [!note]
> Immediately report all damage to storage racks and the building structure to Management.

> [!note]
> The following Attachments are located in the electronic document management system:
> - Attachment A, Monthly Inspection Checklist – Plainfield Only
> - Attachment B, Monthly Inspection Checklist – Enfield Only
> - Attachment E, Scope of Work and Cleaning Materials Plainfield Distribution Center Only
> - Attachment F, Scope of Work and Cleaning Materials Enfield Distribution Center Only

## *Housekeeping

NALO Management ensures the following:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Operations or Maintenance personnel perform monthly inspections of assigned areas (see Attachments A, B, and D). Inspection is documented by circling tasks, initials, and date completed. |
| 2 | Completed forms will be reviewed, signed, and dated by the area supervisor. Completed forms are retained in accordance with the Global Records Retention Schedule. |
| 3 | The contracted cleaning service provider at each site cleans the facility according to the cleaning schedule, using only approved cleaning agents (see Attachments E, F, and G). |
| 4 | Trash is not staged outside of trash receptacles or containers at any time. Trash and debris are removed from the facility and immediate premises on a routine basis. |
| 5 | Cleaning supplies are stored in designated locations.<br>a) Prepared agents must be labeled appropriately and should not be stored in open containers.<br>b) All mopheads, wipes, etc. must be appropriately cleaned, hung to air dry, or discarded after each use.<br>c) Mop buckets must be emptied, cleaned, and dried after each use. |
| 6 | General areas (break rooms, corridors, etc.) always remain free from obstruction(s) and debris. |
| 7 | Employees and contractors wear the proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) required by the directions and/or Safety Data Sheets (SDS) of the cleaning products. |
| 8 | Any special cleaning projects completed in GMP areas are documented using engineering work orders and retained in accordance with the Global Records Retention Schedule. |

> [!note]
> Sites may have Operations personnel perform some or all of these duties instead of a contracted service. *(applies to Step 3)*

> [!note]
> Never allow cleaning supplies or solutions to contact product or product containers. *(applies to Step 7)*

### *Housekeeping, Continued

NALO Management ensures the following:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 8 | Spills are cleaned up immediately, and proper safety signage is utilized when spills are observed. Signs are removed after the spill is cleaned up and the area is completely dry.<br>- Cover spill to prevent product migration.<br>- Call the appropriate local spill clean-up contractor.<br>- Generate Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for review by local spill clean-up contractor. |

> [!note]
> Refer to USD-SOP-Spill Clean Up-001 for spill clean-up.

## Food and Beverage Policy for NALO Warehouses

### General Policy

*Food, beverage, and candy items (e.g., gum, mints, etc.) are not allowed in Designated Screening Areas (DSAs) except for water in a water bottle and/or water jug. DSAs include the entire Plainfield warehouse including office spaces. DSAs for Enfield are only the Controlled Substance (CS) Cage and Vault. Drinking and eating are only permitted in break rooms and office spaces not within DSAs.

Tobacco and vape products of any kind are not allowed anywhere on the NALO properties.
