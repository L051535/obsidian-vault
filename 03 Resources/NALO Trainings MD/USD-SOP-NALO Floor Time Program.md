# NALO Floor Time Program

**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Quality Assurance

## Purpose

Defines requirements for routine Process Team and QA oversight of Operations via the Floor Time Program — fostering operational excellence and quality compliance through ongoing engagement with frontline resources. Covers floor time observations of Lilly-owned facilities only; 3PL warehouses are out of scope.

## References

- CQP-101-4, Floor Time Program

## Reasons for Revision

Revised in its entirety for process improvements.

## Program Overview

Connects frontline staff with Process Team members and QA representatives, documenting active QA presence/oversight.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Quality Floor Time Leader | Owns the Floor Time Program; creates the work plan in the electronic record database; approves Floor Time Observations. |
| Quality Floor Time Leader & Process Team Leader | Categorize operational activities by criticality/complexity/historical performance; set the overall program schedule to cover identified operations yearly; review/approve the electronic-record work plan; ensure activities are on the Process Team agenda; establish the Floor Time Program Plan with the Process Team; ensure improvement opportunities are reviewed/prioritized by the Process Team; ensure conformance and documentation of observations; host a Quality Lead Team review of observations/conformance by end of Q4 annually; update the Observation Plan and create a new work plan annually. |
| Process Team & Quality Floor Time Participants | Schedule/participate in observations with frontline resources (considering timing, space, distractions — area SME participation required); encourage open sharing, identify/remove process barriers, educate on compliance; identify improvement opportunities and share with the Process Team, informing frontline resources of outcomes that affect their work; escalate non-discretionary observations to supervision and Quality. |
| Frontline Resources | Participate in the program; escalate to supervision/Quality anything impacting their ability to complete a task (procedure not executable as written, practice misaligned with procedure). |
| Quality Floor Time Participants | Execute and document observation activities in the electronic record database. |

## Scope, Prioritization, and Plan

**Scope:** aligned with each Process Team's area of responsibility; includes GMP elements (safety, compliance) and non-GMP elements.

**Prioritization:**
1. Structured around frontline operational activities (see Attachment 1 for PDC/EDC activities), classified high/medium/low risk by criticality, complexity, and historical performance.
2. The Quality Floor Time Leader and Process Team Leader select activities based on: **Criticality** (highly critical data, high risk, high potential for product loss/safety incident); **Complexity** (needs training beyond basic, needs a witness/scribe, precision-critical outcomes); **Historical performance** (higher error risk, quality/safety trends such as housekeeping opportunities, quarterly observation trending outcomes).
3. CS and SSS areas should be observed at minimum quarterly, given the high-risk nature of the products.
4. Activities that aren't critical, complex, or historically concerning may be removed from the plan.

**Plan:**
1. Document the plan and rationale in the electronic record database's Activity Planner module.
2. Randomize the observation schedule across days, personnel, and shifts.
3. Set an overall task observation frequency by priority (e.g., monthly) for a regular floor time cadence.
4. The Process Team Leader and QA Floor Time Leader approve the plan in the Activity Planner.
5. The QA Approver ensures rationale is documented — not a detailed risk analysis, just the team's read on whether the driver is Criticality, Complexity, or Historical Performance per task. Out-of-scope activities don't need to be identified.
6. Review/update the plan annually (new plan created each January); changes follow the Activity Planning process. Date changes are acceptable if planned activities still occur during the year and compliance is confirmed at year-end.

## Floor Time Activity

Covers observing identified operational activities, work areas, written instructions, and personnel practice vs. procedure — see Attachment 1 for preparation/execution guidance. *(Attachment 1, Section 4 lists examples only — not required.)* Team behaviors matter: this should be a collaborative information/compliance exchange plus ideas for operational excellence/continuous improvement.

> [!note]
> This program is not an audit — it's meant to drive understanding, collaboration, and improvement opportunities.

## Improvements, Documentation, and Oversight

**Improvement Opportunities:**
1. **Non-discretionary** — potential quality/compliance/health/safety/environmental impact. Prioritized for action; the relevant Process Team/QA member is consulted for next steps (e.g., a Quality Rep for a deviation, an HSE Rep for safety/environmental impact). *A quality system record must be opened to address the compliance concern.*
2. **Discretionary** — the Process Team has latitude to decide whether to pursue it (e.g., wording clarification with no operational/understanding impact).
3. Both types' decisions/actions are documented and reviewed during routine metrics presentations as part of program compliance.

**Documentation** (minimum requirements):
1. Observation Plan overview with rationale (once per team, each January).
2. Each observation execution recorded in a child record: observer name(s)/role, activity date and total time, location/process area, operational task watched, and cross-reference to any associated tracking number (e.g., electronic-record work item number, deviation TR#).

**Oversight:** A Floor Time Program compliance presentation goes to Operations and Quality next-level management at least annually (to the Quality Lead Team), with completion verified via a child record on the work plan. At minimum: total observations planned vs. executed, and a summary of discretionary/non-discretionary findings.

## Appendix A: Observation Plan Template

Example structure each team documents annually in the Activity Planner (one task observed monthly by at least one QA team member):

- **Based on Criticality** (used for repeat observation) — Task 1, Task 2, Task 3, Task 4
- **Based on Complexity** — Task 5
- **Based on recent performance and deviation trending** — Task 6, Task 7
- **Due to the infrequent nature of the task** — Task 8

## Appendix B: Electronic Record Database Activity Planner Structure

The Activity Planner structure ensures proper documentation/tracking of the program's periodic elements; additional Work Items are created as needed (date-focused or task-focused) to align with the Observation Plan. Ensure actions capture:

- Assess and document evidence of conformance to the floor time program and observation plan completion — **due Q4**.
- Provide an annual update on floor time program conformance to the NALO Quality Lead Team (metrics, summary of work items, and any actions) — **due Q4**.
