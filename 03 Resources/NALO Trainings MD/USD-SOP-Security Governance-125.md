# NALO Security Governance Procedure

**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Engineering and Maintenance, NALO Transportation & Logistics, NALO Quality Assurance
**Areas Affected:** Security Operations
**References:** DO23580, Regional DCs Security Post Orders; DO23578, PDC Security Post Orders

## Purpose

To outline the governance structure of security practices and meetings within North American Logistics Operations (NALO).

## Introduction

This document is intended to outline the governance structure for security operations at the NALO Distribution Centers (DC). The employees responsible for ensuring the integrity of security operations comprise a cross-functional team with representatives from Lilly Global Security, NALO Management, Operations, Quality Assurance, Transportation and Contracted Security. Roles and Responsibilities are delineated in RACI charts for Enfield (Attachment A), PDC (Attachment B), and NALO Security Resourcing (Attachment C).

> [!note]
> Attachment C, NALO Security Resourcing, resides in the electronic document management system, and is meant to assign responsibilities for certain security-type tasks. The GMP processes associated with the security tasks are covered in separate NALO procedures.

## Post Orders & Officer Training

The Regional and PDC Post orders are the governing documents which outline the general duties of security officers at each facility and are as follows:
- DO23580, Regional DCs Security Post Orders
- DO23578, PDC Security Post Orders

It is the expectation of NALO that Securitas Security Officers adhere to and follow the directions outlined in these documents. Any changes or updates to the post orders are to be reviewed in the NALO Bi-weekly Security meetings.

Each location is completely staffed with full-time officers to cover each shift, including one Securitas supervisor who is present during regular Lilly business hours. DC Supervision is responsible for ensuring Learning Plans are assigned. Securitas supervision is responsible for ensuring 100% compliance of Learning Plans.

If a situation arises where staffing requirements cannot be met, Lilly and Securitas Leadership coordinates to develop the most effective plan which provides adequate 24/7 coverage for the distribution center.

## Securitas Vision System Daily Activity Reports (DAR) & Daily Security Meetings

Daily Activity Reports are logs of events recorded by security personnel each shift. These logs are reviewed on a daily basis, at a minimum, by each officer during shift change, and the on-duty Securitas Supervisor. A member of the distribution center leadership (i.e., Supervision, Back-Up Supervision, or Management) reviews the DARs regularly for issues. See DO23580, Regional DCs Security Post Orders and DO23578, PDC Security Post Orders.

All DARs are reviewed each week by operations supervision or operations associate at each site and any issues or concerns are discussed with NALO Operations and Engineering Management each week. At a minimum, Lilly Supervision or Management is required to meet with the Securitas Supervisor on a bi-weekly basis to review DARs and ensure that security operations are continuing as normal and that any new or pending issues have been communicated and addressed as necessary.

## Bi-weekly Security Meetings

Communication of security issues and execution of security-related actions are formally managed through bi-weekly meetings. The following members comprise the Security Management Team (SMT) and are responsible for attending and executing NALO Bi-Weekly Security meetings:
- NALO Management of Logistics Operations
- NALO Quality Assurance Management
- NALO International/Domestic Transportation Management
- NALO Engineering Management
- Eli Lilly Global Security Representative
- Quality Assurance Representatives
- Operations Supervision
- Contracted Security Supervision

Topics to be covered in the bi-weekly meetings include:
- Securitas Vision System - DAR Review – PDC and Regions (bi-weekly)
- Local Alarm Trending – PDC and Regions (bi-monthly)

In addition, any pertinent issues observed throughout the week are reviewed in this bi-weekly meeting. The items to be covered include, but are not limited to: Security staffing, burglar alarms, local alarm issues, security breaches, security system impairments/malfunctions/changes, potential security threats, and security support functions.

## Periodic Counter-Surveillance Testing

Counter-Surveillance Testing is a process by which NALO periodically tests the knowledge and reactions of the Securitas Officers to help improve security operations, policies and procedures. These scenarios are discussed by the SMT and executed locally at each distribution center.

For each exercise, local distribution center leadership coordinates with current site security provider, Lilly Global Security, local law enforcement, and Securitas Management prior to deployment. At least 1 exercise is conducted at each site per year, with planning and post-execution follow-up discussed at the bi-weekly security meetings.

## Vendor Management

Quarterly business reviews with Securitas Inc. include the following topics:
- Shift Coverage
- Post Orders, Officer training and qualifications
- Alarm trends and officer response requirements
- Business administration, as needed

Quarterly business reviews with current site security provider include the following topics:
- Service and Support
- Alarm trends
- Technical issues resolution and security system technology
- Business administration, as needed
