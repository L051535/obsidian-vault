# NALO Security Governance Procedure

**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Engineering and Maintenance, NALO Transportation & Logistics, NALO Quality Assurance
**Areas Affected:** Security Operations

## Purpose

To outline the governance structure of security practices and meetings within North American Logistics Operations (NALO).

## References

- DO23580, Regional DCs Security Post Orders
- DO23578, PDC Security Post Orders

## Definitions

**Daily Activity Reports (DARs)** – Electronic logs of events recorded by security personnel each shift. There are separate logs for each site. The system used by Securitas is referred to as "Securitas Vision System".

## Reasons for Revision

To remove Fresno from the procedure and Attachment A. Rationale: CC40755631.

## Introduction

This document outlines the governance structure for security operations at the NALO Distribution Centers (DC). The employees responsible for ensuring the integrity of security operations comprise a cross-functional team with representatives from Lilly Global Security, NALO Management, Operations, Quality Assurance, Transportation and Contracted Security. Roles and Responsibilities are delineated in RACI charts for Enfield (Attachment A), PDC (Attachment B), and NALO Security Resourcing (Attachment C).

> [!note]
> Attachment C, NALO Security Resourcing, resides in the electronic document management system, and is meant to assign responsibilities for certain security-type tasks. The GMP processes associated with the security tasks are covered in separate NALO procedures.

## Post Orders & Officer Training

The Regional and PDC Post orders are the governing documents which outline the general duties of security officers at each facility:

- DO23580, Regional DCs Security Post Orders
- DO23578, PDC Security Post Orders

NALO expects Securitas Security Officers to adhere to and follow the directions outlined in these documents. Any changes or updates to the post orders are reviewed in the NALO Bi-weekly Security meetings.

Each location is completely staffed with full-time officers to cover each shift, including one Securitas supervisor present during regular Lilly business hours. DC Supervision is responsible for ensuring Learning Plans are assigned. Securitas supervision is responsible for ensuring 100% compliance of Learning Plans.

If a situation arises where staffing requirements cannot be met, Lilly and Securitas Leadership coordinate to develop the most effective plan providing adequate 24/7 coverage for the distribution center.

## Securitas Vision System Daily Activity Reports (DAR) & Daily Security Meetings

DARs are logs of events recorded by security personnel each shift. These logs are reviewed on a daily basis, at a minimum, by each officer during shift change, and the on-duty Securitas Supervisor. A member of distribution center leadership (Supervision, Back-Up Supervision, or Management) reviews the DARs regularly for issues. See DO23580 and DO23578.

All DARs are reviewed each week by operations supervision or an operations associate at each site, with issues or concerns discussed with NALO Operations and Engineering Management each week. At a minimum, Lilly Supervision or Management meets with the Securitas Supervisor bi-weekly to review DARs and ensure security operations are continuing as normal, and that any new or pending issues have been communicated and addressed.

## Bi-weekly Security Meetings

Communication of security issues and execution of security-related actions are formally managed through bi-weekly meetings. The Security Management Team (SMT) responsible for attending and executing NALO Bi-Weekly Security meetings includes:

- NALO Management of Logistics Operations
- NALO Quality Assurance Management
- NALO International/Domestic Transportation Management
- NALO Engineering Management
- Eli Lilly Global Security Representative
- Quality Assurance Representatives
- Operations Supervision
- Contracted Security Supervision

Topics covered: Securitas Vision System DAR Review – PDC and Regions (bi-weekly); Local Alarm Trending – PDC and Regions (bi-monthly); plus any pertinent issues from the week, including security staffing, burglar alarms, local alarm issues, security breaches, security system impairments/malfunctions/changes, potential security threats, and security support functions.

## Periodic Counter-Surveillance Testing

Counter-Surveillance Testing periodically tests the knowledge and reactions of Securitas Officers to help improve security operations, policies and procedures. Scenarios are discussed by the SMT and executed locally at each distribution center.

For each exercise, local distribution center leadership coordinates with the current site security provider, Lilly Global Security, local law enforcement, and Securitas Management prior to deployment. At least 1 exercise is conducted at each site per year, with planning and post-execution follow-up discussed at the bi-weekly security meetings.

## Vendor Management

Quarterly business reviews with Securitas Inc. cover: Shift Coverage; Post Orders, Officer training and qualifications; Alarm trends and officer response requirements; Business administration, as needed.

Quarterly business reviews with the current site security provider cover: Service and Support; Alarm trends; Technical issues resolution and security system technology; Business administration, as needed.

## Attachment A — RACI Chart for Enfield

RACI chart covering Fire Protection, Security System (incl. access control), CCTV System, NVR Workstation, Cameras, Network, Detection (motion/glass break/vibration), Security system wiring, Electrical System, Panic Alarm System, and Fire Alarm System — assigning Responsible/Accountable/Consulted/Informed roles across Site security provider, NALO Engineering, FSS System Engineers (Fire/Security/CCTV), FSS Manager, Regional Operations Management, NALO Engineering/Maintenance Management, Lilly Server Support, Electrical contractor (FDC = Wild Ele, EDC = Advanced Electrical Services), Lilly IT GMS Infrastructure, and Tech Center FP/Security Engineer.

## Attachment B — RACI Chart for PDC

Similar RACI chart structure for PDC covering Fire Protection, Security System, CCTV System, motion/glass-break detection (PMs only), Panic Alarm, Fire Alarm, Public Address, and Network, across FSS Engineers, NALO Engineering/Maintenance, Site security provider, Regions Management, Lilly IT GMS Infrastructure, and Tech Center FP/Security Engineer roles.
