# Security in and Access to Facilities, SSS, and CS Areas in North American Logistics Operations

**Supersedes:** 001-001391
**Areas Involved:** North American Logistics Operations (NALO), and support areas

## Purpose

To define requirements for security and alarm responses, and to provide traceable security of distribution facilities — specifically Lilly-designated Special Security Substances (SSS)/Controlled Substance (CS) area(s).

## Introduction

All product distribution areas storing drug products, packaging materials, and/or semi-finished materials maintain a security alarm system to detect unauthorized entry (business hours and after) and limit entry to approved personnel. Employee/visitor/after-hours access must be controlled and documented per Global Quality Standards, Global Security Standards, and Lilly Quality Standards.

The PDC Escort Policy and PDC Escort Site Access Request are on the NALO collaboration site under NALO Site Policies. Print attachments from the electronic document management system each time they're used, to ensure the correct version. All loose-leaf logs need page numbers at the bottom showing year and page number (e.g., 2019-1, 2019-2).

## General Rules

1. Purses/backpacks and personal coats/jackets/hoodies with pockets aren't allowed in Designated Screening Areas (DSAs) — which can include the entire warehouse, office spaces, Maintenance Shops, CS Cages, Vaults, etc. Personal items must stay in lockers.
2. If a coat/jacket is needed for work, the company provides one. Company-issued jackets/coats/coveralls/vests may not be worn outside during the workday (incl. breaks/lunch) unless the job requires outdoor work (e.g., Dock, Maintenance).
3. Local area management/supervision must approve SSS area access; employees must be authorized to handle controlled substances per GQAAC-PRD-124560.
4. Storing non-controlled substances with controlled substances requires local DEA office approval and proper documentation before comingling.
5. Unauthorized/unapproved/untrained personnel must be escorted by approved personnel in limited access areas.
6. The PDC warehouse area is a DSA with exclusive access — special clearance is required beforehand.
7. All unauthorized individuals must be escorted at all times throughout warehouse areas; the escort cannot perform other duties while escorting.
8. Tour groups are capped at 10 individuals; the tour escort can't perform other duties and must maintain visual line of sight on each person.
9. Master security access lists per facility must be maintained (in Genetec, Lilly's corporate security software). Contractor access to external gates/entrances is reduced as approved by facility leadership; if access is needed outside reduced hours, facility leadership proactively provides security personnel a list. Lists must be accurate/current, reviewed at least quarterly by management/supervision per SEC-STD-200. Lilly Global Security handles badge activation/deactivation — Genetec add/delete access is managed at the corporate FSS level; no site personnel have that administrative access.
10. Security passwords/codes must be changed annually if shared; otherwise changed on personnel change or suspected compromise.
11. **PIT Security** — forklifts without passcodes must have the key removed and secured in a locked cabinet/desk at end of shift.
12. **EDC only** — anyone entering the Enfield CS cage to pick/process orders must be accompanied by another CS-authorized employee the entire time; the door must be closed/secured on entry/exit, and the area alarm reactivated once the warehouse is secured. Card-reader users must badge in/out each pass. For maintenance, a lone CS-authorized employee may escort the Contractor/Technician — if no card access, they sign Attachment D (Enfield CS Cage Access Entry Log) per LQP303-2. After-hours EDC CS cage access requires Supervision/Management approval (or delegate per USD-SOP-Deleg of Approval-001) beforehand — see CS-SOP-13757 and CS-FRM-13757-01. (After-hours = overtime not an extension of the regular business day, e.g. a Saturday or a callback.)
13. **Trailer and Envirotainer Security Seals** — numbered seals stay in a locked cabinet; remove only when needed for a shipment, and record seal numbers per USD-SOP-Loading Temp Controlled Trucks-001.

## Facility Security and Fire Monitoring

NALO Management/Supervision prevent unauthorized entry or product removal during trash pickup:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Keep access to drug-product storage facilities to a minimum. |
| 2 | Maintain adequate perimeter lighting so security camera images are viewable by monitoring personnel. |
| 3 | Maintain a security alarm system deterring after-hours entry and protecting against theft/diversion. |
| 4 | Protect against theft/diversion facilitated or hidden by tampering with computers or electronic records. |
| 5 | Observe external trash removal from all drug product/packaging/semi-finished storage areas; ensure pickup areas are camera-monitored or inspected daily to detect/deter theft. |
| 6 | Ensure periodic security system testing — Lilly Security keeps PDC's testing records; EDC keeps its records on site. |
| 7 | Lilly Security monitors fire/security alarms and notifies police/fire and appropriate Lilly personnel. *At EDC, the alarm system transmits to the contracted alarm service, which notifies police/fire/Lilly personnel.* |

## Facility Access – All Distribution Centers

Access depends on which part of the facility (warehouse/office/grounds), the requester type (employee/contractor/visitor), and badge/access status. Detailed requirements are in Attachment E (PDC) and Attachment F (EDC) — see the condensed summary below.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Visitors/personnel without badges sign in (Attachment A, Visitor Log) per the Facility Access Matrices — a valid government/company ID confirms their real name (not required after the first visit if the escort already knows them). Signed-in personnel must sign out and return any issued visitor badge on exit. After-hours entry needs NALO Management/Supervision approval and alarm service/security notification. *(Attachment A's required fields are fixed; format/layout is flexible.)* |
| 2 | Trucks must have driver ID checked/recorded before facility-grounds access: Small Package carriers (UPS/FedEx) and Non-Pharma — driver's license or company ID; Inbound Pharma — driver's license or company ID; Outbound Pharma — driver's license via the Pre-alert process. Driving teams: both drivers must be present and verified. |

## Designated Screening Areas (DSA)

PDC has three areas — Office, Mechanical Rooms, and Warehouse (the Warehouse Area is the DSA). At EDC, the CS cage and vault areas are the DSAs. Only authorized personnel (those who passed a Security Threat Assessment, STA) get unescorted DSA admittance — contact the Facility Security Coordinator or NALO Security Coordinator for STA info. *Security officers keep a current TSA-authorized-individual list for access purposes, maintained by the Facility Security Coordinator.*

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Badges must be worn at all times. Ensure only authorized individuals enter the DSA: Lilly ID → scan the card reader; door held open after a break → scan to confirm a green light; temporary ID → check for the DSA-approval marking; badge doesn't unlock the door/shows a red light → do not admit without an escort (escort required throughout the DSA); contract maintenance personnel without a Lilly badge → must be escorted, but with a temporary badge stamped for DSA access, entry is allowed during normal business hours. |
| 3 | Verify card-reader access at entry points is appropriate; grant/remove as needed so only approved/authorized personnel have SSS/CS card access. *Local management/supervision decides who gets SSS-area card access; access is requested via MyAccess with proof of training to the Security Coordinator/Genetec admin. SSS/CS card access lists are reviewed at least quarterly (with the master list, per SEC-STD-200) and at minimum annually per LQS303, documented on the actual Genetec reports.* |
| 4 | Review card-access system reports quarterly for irregularities/misuse (repeated failed attempts, false readings, disabled cards, card sharing), per SEC-STD-200. |
| 5 | Challenge unfamiliar/unauthorized individuals in the DSA: ask to see their ID badge, whether they completed an STA with the Facility Security Coordinator, who the site's Facility Security Coordinator is, and where their escort is. |
| 6 | If an unauthorized individual is found unescorted in the DSA: escort them immediately to a supervisor/manager/Facility Security Coordinator; report to the Facility Security Coordinator; confirm cargo being screened has no unauthorized substances; search the DSA to confirm nothing was introduced to screened cargo. If unauthorized persons/devices can't be resolved, contact the Facility/NALO Security Coordinator to notify TSA. |

## Facility Access – Enfield

The NALO Security Coordinator or Lead Security (M1) at Enfield grants/removes Genetec card-reader access. *Keep active only employees who are NALO resources or provide repeated site support; Corporate Security activates/deactivates badges.*

**Grant or Remove Access:** Log into Genetec → Cardholder Management → search by name → select modify → use "+"/"×" on Cardholder Group to add/remove access → select the needed access level → save.

**Monthly Badge Access Review:** Log into Genetec → Security Desk → Cardholder Configuration → advanced search → select Enfield Cardholder group(s) → Generate Report → trim columns to Cardholder/First Name/Last Name/Picture/Cardholder groups → verify access is correct for each group (grant/deactivate/remove as needed) → print, sign, and file with Attachment G (Regional Distribution Center Card Access Review Form).

**Badge Scan Review:** Log into Genetec → Security Desk → Door Activities → select review criteria (doors, events, timeframe) → Generate Report → trim columns to Event/Door/Side/Event Timestamp/First Name/Last Name/Credential → review at minimum monthly for the CS cage and quarterly for the rest of the storage area, checking for irregularities/misuse. *Per USD-SOP-CS Inventory, Auth, and Access Control Review-165, Enfield CS cage access is reviewed monthly; non-CS quarterly review is per SEC-STD-200; SSS/CS review is at minimum yearly per LQS303.* Initial/date Attachment G (explain any blanks per Good Documentation Practices). Non-CS reports aren't printed due to file size, but CS-cage reports are printed; file printed reports and the signed Attachment G in GMP files.

## Lilly Representative — Alarm Response

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Respond to alarms per USD-SOP-Regions Security and Fire Alarm Response-080 (and CS-SOP-13757 for CS-cage alarms). **Do not enter the facility** until security confirms it's safe, per that same procedure. For temperature alarms, follow USD-SOP-Temp Reqmnts, Alarm Resp & Eval and MKT-001. Respond immediately when called by the contracted security officer or Lilly Security, any time. If Police/Fire are on site, wait in the parking lot for their instructions; for out-of-hours alarms, wait in the parking area for them and investigate only under their direction/accompaniment. |
| 2 | After the all-clear, investigate for tampering/forced entry/possible theft. Notify Center Management/Supervision, Corporate Security, and NALO Operations Management if found. SSS-area involvement → notify that area's Supervision/Management immediately. CS-area involvement → follow CS-SOP-13757. DSA involvement → notify the Facility/NALO Security Coordinator. *If the alarm is false, clear the area and reset — but notify the alarm service/Lilly Security before resetting to confirm system status.* |
| 3 | Monitor the facility: stay until resolved (re-secure and reset if possible; if not, move SSS to alternate secure storage and document quantities transferred); sweep the DSA if involved; contact police for extra monitoring if needed; use an outside security agency if needed; contact Global Security for guidance if needed; notify the alarm service before resetting; reference USD-SOP-Deviation Mgmt-001 to determine if the event needs documenting. |
| 4 | If appropriate, conduct a physical inventory to check for/quantify missing product. If product is missing, notify Global Security, NALO QA, and GQAAC Compliance/CS ASAP during business hours — see USD-SOP-Emergency Inventory Process-085 for the post-theft/loss inventory process. |

## Access to Special Security Substance / Controlled Substance Areas

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| SSS-approved/CS-authorized individual with card-reader access | Enter via card reader — no log entry required. *EDC only: minimum 2 authorized personnel in the CS cage at once; badge out when leaving.* |
| SSS-approved/CS-authorized individual without card-reader access | Must be escorted. *EDC only: complete Attachment D (Enfield CS Cage Access Entry Log).* |

## Attachment E — PDC Facility Access Matrix, and Attachment F — EDC Facility Access Matrix

> [!note]
> These two attachments are large reference tables (6 pages and 3 pages respectively in the source PDF) covering, per requester type (Lilly employee, Lilly contract employee, non-Lilly visitor, contractor, government agency), the exact procedure for: badged with/without card-reader access, unbadged, badge forgotten/lost, vehicle/parking access, and exterior-grounds-only access. The pattern is consistent enough to summarize here, but for the exact wording per scenario/site, refer to the source PDF/QualityDocs directly.

**General pattern (PDC and EDC alike):**
- **Badged with card-reader access** → the reader itself controls entry; no additional log entry needed (EDC: non-Lilly visitors don't get card access).
- **Badged without card-reader access, or forgot/lost badge** → contacted host (or Security Officer, at EDC) verifies identity (in person or via camera), issues a temporary badge, and logs the person in a sign-in book — lost badges also trigger a call to Global Security to deactivate the old badge and start a replacement.
- **Visitors, unbadged personnel, and government agency representatives** → always sign the facility visitor log, wear a visitor badge, and are escorted by an authorized host at all times while in the warehouse (PDC: with a narrow exception for restroom breaks, where the escort waits outside; EDC office areas only require supervision, not a full-time escort).
- **Vehicle/parking access** — PDC restricts vehicle access to designated painted spaces near the guardhouse (South/East end) for Security/Supervisors/QA, with separate rules for Maintenance parking (North/East end) and a 25-meter no-parking zone around dock doors; contractors need verbal/written manager approval to bring a vehicle inside the perimeter. EDC requires facility Management approval for any dock-area parking and enforces the same 25-meter dock clearance.
- **Government agencies (FDA, EPA, IDEM, TSA, DOT, OSHA, DEA, etc.)** must always present government-issued ID and be escorted by PDC/EDC management or a designated host per the site contact list.
