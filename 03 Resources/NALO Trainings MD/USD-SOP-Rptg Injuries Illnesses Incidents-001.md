# Reporting On-the-Job Injuries, Illnesses, and Near Miss Incidents

**Supersedes:** 001-002580, Training Course DOHS007
**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations and support areas

## Purpose

To instruct North American Logistics Operations (NALO) employees how to report on-the-job injuries, illnesses, and near miss incidents. Also, to explain how the incident reporting and investigation requirements included in this procedure relate to the overall investigation process.

## References

- USD-SOP-Deviation Mgmt-001

## Definitions

- **Health and Safety Incidents** – Injuries or near misses that can result from an Event (i.e., out of the ordinary incident). Examples include personal injury, illness, property damage and facility evacuations.
- **Illness** – Any abnormal condition or disorder, other than one resulting from an injury, caused by exposure to environmental factors.
- **Investigation** – The follow-up process that occurs after an incident that can include interview(s) to gather facts, root cause analysis, corrective actions, communication of facts, and documentation. Investigations also help determine work-relatedness and OSHA recordability status of an injury/illness.
- **Injury** – A case where a person was hurt as a result of the incident and needed to report for medical attention.
- **Near Miss** – A mishap that could have (but did not) result in personal injury. May also have the potential to cause serious damage to (Lilly Assets) property, equipment, or the environment (e.g., potential for explosion, fire, chemical release).
- **OSHA-Recordable Injury/Illness** – All work-related deaths and illnesses, and work-related injuries which result in loss of consciousness, restriction of work or motion, transfer to another job, and/or medical treatment beyond first aid.

## Introduction

Any injury, illness or near miss incident that occurs at work or may be related to the workplace must be reported immediately. The two main benefits of immediate reporting are: (1) care from medical professionals can reduce the chance of an injury becoming more serious, and (2) immediate investigation can begin.

Even minor injuries must be reported to your supervision. If unsure about the severity and need for treatment of an injury, report and discuss the incident with your supervision. You must also report to your supervision if you use material from a company-provided First Aid Kit. Examples of minor injuries include feeling a pull or hearing a pop in your back with no immediate pain, and minor cuts.

NALO investigates all injury/illness and near miss incidents to learn from them (preventing future similar incidents) and to ensure accurate determinations and recordkeeping.

> [!note]
> Employees in safety critical jobs must report to EHS if taking any potentially sedating medication. EHS must consider the employee's job-related tasks and usage of the medication. Safety critical jobs are determined locally by HSE and must be communicated to affected employees.

## Reporting On-the-Job Injuries and Illnesses

> [!warning]
> Delays in reporting on-the-job incidents (injury, illness or near miss) can prevent effective incident investigation.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Inform Supervision of the injury/illness immediately, even if it appears minor. Supervision will immediately communicate injury/illness to Management. |
| 2 | Determine next steps as a team with NALO HSE Representative and area management — see decision table below. |

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Injury is very minor and first aid can be administered (e.g., a paper cut requiring a band-aid) | Employee administers first aid from the first aid kit; signs on to EHS collaboration site and clicks "Report a Work Related Injury/Illness"; completes the electronic form submitted to EHS; Supervision initiates a CAPA/Trackwise event. |
| Injury is ergonomic in nature | Supervision contacts athletic trainer and area Management for notification; athletic trainer follows up with employee and contacts site supervision with recommended next steps; site supervision, NALO HSE Representative, and athletic trainer agree on path forward and communicate with employee. (If athletic trainer cannot be reached, contact the NALO HSE Representative.) |
| Any other type of injury or illness | Go to designated health care provider for medical treatment and recordkeeping; deliver any paperwork to supervision; follow the health care provider's recommendations. |

**Designated Health Care Providers by site:**

| Site | Designated Health Care Provider |
|---|---|
| Enfield, CT / Fresno, CA / Indianapolis, IN | Concentra Medical Center |
| Plainfield, IN | Lilly Employee Health Services and/or Hendricks Regional Health Occupational Medicine Center |

## Reporting Near Miss Incidents

> [!warning]
> Delays in reporting on-the-job incidents (injury, illness or near miss) can prevent effective incident investigation.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immediately inform supervision of the near miss. If applicable, do not move equipment, materials, tools, etc. which were involved in the incident. |
| 2 | Assist the investigation process by sharing information about the incident and participating in resulting root cause analysis actions. |

## Documenting On-the-Job Injuries, Illnesses, and Near Miss Incidents

Supervision documents reported on-the-job injuries, illnesses, and near miss incidents within the Trackwise Event Management system. Refer to USD-SOP-Deviation Mgmt-001 for these instructions.

## Attachment A — Investigation Process Flowchart

Overview of the reporting/investigation process:
1. HSE Event Occurs → determine if it's an Injury/Illness or Near Miss.
2. **Near Miss** → Record and investigate per Lilly CAPA/Trackwise system.
3. **Injury/Illness** → Is it work related? If No → no TR required, accommodate employee as needed.
4. If Yes → Is it Ergonomic Related? If Yes → work with Athletic Trainer per this procedure.
5. If No → Is it very minor and can be treated locally? If Yes → work with NALO HSE Representative to administer first aid, document per this procedure.
6. If No → employee goes to healthcare provider for treatment and recordkeeping. Healthcare provider generates an Injury/Illness form (at Indianapolis/Plainfield, Employee Health Service generates the form; at Regional Centers, Supervision/Management and/or the employee generates it electronically, consulting NALO HSE Staff if needed).
7. Injury/Illness form is electronically delivered to the Indianapolis Occupational Health & Safety Dept. and the NALO Health and Safety Staff → recorded and investigated per Lilly CAPA/Trackwise system.
8. Final determinations: Site OSHA Recordkeeper utilizes Injury/Illness form investigation(s), healthcare provider information, paperwork, and forms to make final determinations on injury/illness, work-relatedness, and OSHA recordability.

> [!note]
> Reference the Global Health and Safety Standard for additional Injury/Illness Case Management guidance and details.
