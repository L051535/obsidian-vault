# Trend Triage

**Supersedes:** GSOP-104-7-TL-1, v2
**Associated Procedure:** GSOP-104-7

## Purpose

Describes the process of creating and executing Trend Triage records within the Quality Incident (QI) module of the Global Deviation Database (GDD).

## Scope

- **In Scope:** Potential trends identified in Periodic Deviation Monitoring Reports (PDMR), ad hoc Deviation Monitoring, and Quality Deviation Emerging Themes, needing further assessment to determine if a deviation is necessary or whether there's a process-improvement opportunity.
- **Out of Scope:** Trends identified outside GDD. Issues not considered suspected trends.

## 1.0 Process Overview

Suspected trends may come from in-deviation recurrence checks (emerging themes), ad hoc monitoring of deviation data, or PDMR. All identified suspected trends are documented in the Quality Incident module as a Trend Triage record to assess whether further investigation is needed.

## 2.0 Create the Trend Triage Record

| Step | Activity | Related Information | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create the QI Record from Emerging Themes or Ad Hoc Monitoring | Condition: creating a Trend Triage record type in the QI module. Must be created within 1 business day of identifying the issue; gets an automatic 2-calendar-day due date from creation. | Initiator |
| 2 | Create the QI Record from PDMR | Condition: documenting a PDMR as a Trend Triage. Create as soon as possible after completing the PDMR analysis and data gathering. | Initiator |
| 3 | Assign the Trend Assessment Team | Assign team members who will assess whether a deviation is required. | Initiator |
| 4 | Attach Validated Data to the Record | Condition: PDMR/ad hoc monitoring/recurrence-check trends under evaluation. Attach the data (PDMR report, validated Veeva reports, or recurring deviation record numbers) plus a summary of insights and evaluation outcome. | Owner |
| 5 | SME Input Required | Condition: an optional SME is added to the team. SME adds information/insights to support the triage. | Owner, Optional SME |
| 6 | Conduct the Review Prior to Assessment | Review the record for completeness/accuracy; update as needed with supporting pictures/attachments. | Owner |

## 3.0 Conduct the Trend Triage Assessment

| Step | Activity | Related Information | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete the Triage Results | Review the record and select one outcome: "No Trend – No Action Required," "No Trend – CI Required," or "Trend – Deviation Required." | Assessor |
| 2 | Request Additional Information | Condition: the record is incomplete or more information is needed. Mark additional information required — returns to the Owner to update. | Assessor |
| 3 | Complete the Assessment with "No Trend – No Action Required" | Condition: no trend confirmed, no further action needed. Provide justification and close the record. | Assessor |
| 4 | Complete the Assessment with "No Trend – CI Required" | Condition: no trend confirmed, but Continuous Improvement (CI) opportunities identified. If a CI is already in progress, relate it; otherwise a new CI record is created automatically with the Assessor as owner. See GSOP-1102.2 (QMS Continuous Improvement). | Assessor |
| 5 | Complete the Assessment with "Trend – Deviation Required" | Condition: trend confirmed, or assessment inconclusive and further investigation needed. If a deviation is already in progress, relate it; otherwise a new deviation is created automatically with the Assessor as Fact Finder. See GSOP-104-1 (Record Creation & Fact Finding). | Assessor |
