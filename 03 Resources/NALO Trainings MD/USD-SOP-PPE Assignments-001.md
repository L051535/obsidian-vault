# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Assignments for North American Logistics Operations

**Supersedes:** 001-002750, DOHS014
**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, NALO Quality Assurance, and NALO support areas including security guards for Plainfield, Enfield, and Fresno

## Purpose

To instruct North American Logistics Operations (NALO) employees in Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) assignments related to warehouse and maintenance operations.

## Introduction

All levels of management and supervision within NALO are committed to the creation of an injury-free work environment. Employees' safety knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors are critical for the success of this objective.

Lilly intends to design, construct, and operate facilities to eliminate job hazards that may cause personal injury. Where it is not feasible to eliminate such hazards, NALO employees are required to wear PPE to protect against hazards.

This procedure is formatted utilizing work groups within NALO to categorize PPE assignments for each work group. Each NALO employee should reference sections that include their specific work group to fully identify PPE assigned to their specific job tasks.

## Hazard Assessments

1. PPE Hazard assessments were conducted and documented for existing tasks.
2. The HSE staff member maintains records of these hazard assessments.
3. PPE Hazard Assessments for new or changing tasks are required and can be prompted by: CIR H&S Questionnaire, NALO Launch Action List (new product/process), TrackWise Change Management, Addition of new equipment, Impact Assessment Tool (IAT), etc.

## General PPE Instructions

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Obtain PPE from designated storage locations. See area supervision for location of PPE storage. |
| 2 | Inspect all PPE, including fall protection equipment, prior to every use. |
| 3 | When applicable, clean PPE as needed using mild soap and water and allow to air dry. |
| 4 | Store all PPE in a clean, dry location. |
| 5 | To request new or additional PPE, or for hands-on instruction or questions, contact a safety representative. |
| 6 | Reusable PPE may be returned to storage provided it is free of damage, unsoiled, and still ready for use. PPE that is soiled, chemically contaminated, or damaged shall be disposed and may not be stored in the PPE storage locations. |
| 7 | PPE damaged or soiled by a chemical substance shall be disposed based on the substance and PPE's chemical characteristics. |

> [!note]
> PPE including contaminated clothing are not solid wastes unless regulated under the "contained in" policy (US EPA regulation in CFR 261.3(f)(2)). Under this policy, PPE/clothing must be disposed as hazardous waste if it exhibits a characteristic or contains a listed waste. If contamination cannot be removed, the PPE must be treated to land disposal restriction treatment standards prior to disposal. Consult HSE representative with questions.

**Disposal Guide for PPE:**

| Type of PPE | Soiled/Damaged By | Disposal Method |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrile gloves, Cut resistant gloves | M-cresol containing chemicals | Put into the m-cresol waste drum |
| Apron, Cloth gloves, Safety glasses/goggles, Face Shield | Dust, dirt, cardboard dust, water; wear/tear | Regular trash bin |
| Lab coat | Oil/grease | Oily rag waste container |
| Leather gloves | Battery Acid | Regular trash bin |
| Cryogenic gloves, Butyl rubber gloves, Bump Cap/Hard Hat | Paint (acrylic, water based, latex); Pure Temp 4 (orange popsicle packing material) | Regular trash bin |

## Basic/Minimal Safe Clothing on Warehouse Floor

- Steel toed shoes/boots, toe caps, or overshoes with toe caps.
- Full length pants such as cargo pants, flame retardant workwear, and jeans.
- Shirts that cover the ball of the shoulder.
- Basic clothing free of holes to eliminate the exposure of bare skin.
- No sweatpants, leggings, shorts, or open heeled/toed shoes are permitted.

> [!note]
> Exceptions to this basic/minimal clothing policy must be reviewed and approved by Management.

## Broken or Spilled Materials

Broken or spilled materials are cleaned up in accordance with USD-SOP-Spill Clean Up-001.

## PPE Assignment for Employees & Visitors That Enter a NALO Warehouse

Employees and visitors, including tour groups, that enter the PDC, Enfield and Fresno warehouses wear safety shoes and High Visibility shirts or vests per the table below.

> [!note]
> PDC employees/visitors are not required to wear safety shoes or High Visibility shirts/vests when staying within the marked walk path on the south perimeter wall of the warehouse (behind the guardrail). Enfield and Fresno employees/visitors are not required to wear this PPE when staying in marked designated areas. See Attachments A, B, and C.

| Task | Description | PPE |
|---|---|---|
| Walk in a warehouse | Pedestrian travel throughout the warehouse | Steel toed shoes/boots, toe caps, or overshoes with toe caps; High Visibility shirts or vests; basic/minimal safe clothing per above. |

| Task | Description | PPE |

## Non-Routine Hazards

1. A request may prompt a new non-routine activity — a pre-job hazard evaluation should be performed before the task begins.
2. Supervision assigning, and employees performing, non-routine tasks evaluate hazards, consider appropriate PPE use, and consider safe work practice controls such as lockout, fire hazard permits, and confined space entry.
3. Supervision contacts the HSE staff member to review and finalize hazard identification and control for non-routine job tasks.
4. Ask supervision questions related to non-routine tasks.

> [!note]
> No employee is expected to perform any task he/she considers unsafe that has not been assessed by a safety representative, nor without proper PPE supplied to perform the task.

## Spotting for Elevated Lifts

Any personnel, including security guards and contractors, shall wear an approved hard hat per ANSI Z89.1-2009 while spotting for lifts while they are elevated and occupied. Spotting advises or routes traffic around the lift to minimize fall hazard risk associated with supplies or tools; the hard hat protects the spotter in this role.
