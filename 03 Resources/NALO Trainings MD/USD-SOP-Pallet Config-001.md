# Pallet Configuration Requirements in NALO

**Supersedes:** 001-001280
**Areas Involved:** NALO Operations, Contract Manufacturing Quality Assurance, Indianapolis Device Assembly and Packaging (IDAP), IDAP Logistics, Fegersheim Logistics (Shipping)

## Purpose

To outline appropriate pallet configurations for shipments into North American Logistics Operations (NALO).

> [!note]
> This procedure should be considered when negotiating contract manufacturer and Lilly affiliate contracts. Exceptions to the pallet configuration requirements (e.g., use of euro-pallets) must be pre-approved by NALO Operations management.

## Definitions

- **Sample Product** – Product designated as a 'Professional Package' and not intended for sale. All material numbers starting with 'ND' are samples, as well as several other material number combinations.
- **Trade Product** – Product intended for commercial sale.

## Sample Product Pallet Configuration Requirements

> [!note]
> Applies only to non-chill sample items; does not apply to Apollo. Sample items distributed in less than case quantities may instead meet the Trade Product requirements — contact NALO Operations to determine distribution quantity.

| Regarding | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Pallet Characteristics | Comply with ISPM 15 pallet treatment requirements. Pallets from Lilly sites must be Heat Treated. Pallet must be clean, in good condition, no ownership printing. Two-way standard American sized pallet, approx. 42 x 48 inches. |
| Case Characteristics | Cases must be large enough for the conveyor (min. recommended 9"L x 6"W). Max outer case dimensions: 25.25"L x 19.125"W x 20.25"H, 28 lbs. |
| Pallet Pattern | Maintain minimal overhang. Label visible on each side of the pallet. |
| Labeling | Case label required on each case: material number, batch number, expiration date (no product name). |
| Configured Pallets | Total height ≤ 51" (including pallet). Must stack 4 pallets high in bulk storage (Blister product from IDAP only can be stacked 4 high). Full pallets ≤ 700 lbs. |

## Trade Product Pallet Configuration Requirements

> [!note]
> Use local site manufacturing procedures for site pallet requirements, if applicable.

| Regarding | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Pallet Characteristics | Comply with ISPM 15. Pallets from Lilly sites must be Heat Treated. Clean, good condition, no ownership printing. Two-way standard American pallet (~42x48"), euro pallet (~47.25x31.5"), or hybrid pallet (39x47"). Other options require NALO Operations pre-approval. |
| Case Characteristics | Cases large enough for conveyor (min. recommended 9"L x 6"W). Max outer case dimensions: 25.25"L x 19.125"W x 20.25"H, 28 lbs. |
| Pallet Pattern | No overhang; center product on pallet. One or more case labels visible on each side. |
| Outer Packaging | Case label required: material number, batch number, expiration date. For contract-manufactured/affiliate products not shipped in sealed transportation, all cases must be fully covered by outer packaging for tamper evidence and theft deterrence. |
| Hazardous Contents | Must comply with 49 CFR transportation regulations for weight/volume if hazardous for highway transport. |
| Configured Pallets | Total height ≤ 48" (including pallet). Not always applicable for active containers (e.g., LD9 Opticooler, E1, E2) — general rule: pallets should not be taller than 52". Full pallets ≤ 1800 lbs. |

## Pallet Patterns for All Product

- Exceptions to pallet configuration requirements must be pre-approved by NALO Operations Management.
- Only one pallet pattern can be maintained in SAP/WM per item code.
- Once a pallet pattern is submitted to NALO, it may not be changed without prior NALO Operations Management approval.
- Pallets not meeting criteria may be returned to the supplying area for re-configuration.
- International/Export product can ship on a hybrid pallet (39" x 47") up to 48" high; not double stacked.

## Packing Specifications for Export EF2700/2700SWPEL ISC Container

| ISC | Requirements |
|---|---|
| MX6196 - 2700SWPEL (with gel packs) | Internal payload: L=35.25", W=28", H=31.25" |
| MX6196 - EF2700 (no gel packs) | Internal dimensions per manufacturer spec: L=38.5", W=31.75", H=37" |
| NALO Operations Requirements | Pallet/loading patterns must be approved by NALO management prior to changes. Patterns must allow safe/easy incorporation of gel packs to maintain temperature requirements. Product must be packed to minimize shifting in transit. |
| MX1400 | Internal payload: L=42", W=48", H=45" |
