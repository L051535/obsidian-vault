# Change Control

**Associated Standard:** LQS-1400.1 (Change Control), HSE-STD-100 (HSE Management System)

> [!warning]
> This document's status is **Pending Effective** — no effective date has been set yet. Verify current status in QualityDocs before relying on this.

## Purpose

Describes the end-to-end Change Control (CC) process, including documentation within the electronic Quality Management System (eQMS), governance, and oversight expectations — a consistent, global approach aligned to LQS-1400.1 and HSE-STD-100.

## Scope

Applies to all GxP and Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) relevant CCs entered in eQMS — covering product/data quality and safety/efficacy information for pharmaceuticals and medical devices Lilly shares or is legally/regulatorily responsible for, including: associated data/information, GLP, GQP, GPP, GVP, GCP, GDP, GMP, and regulatory authorization.

**Out of Scope:** Execution of a change within the Global Service Management Tool.

Change Control is a structured, risk-based approach for transitioning individuals, teams, and sites/functions from a current state to a desired future state.

## 1.0 Change Control Governance

Users of eQMS must be suitably trained for the tasks they perform.

**Table 1 — Roles and Responsibilities:**

| Change Control Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Change Owner | Manages Change Control from identification through completion — documenting the proposed change, facilitating/summarizing impact assessment(s), and assigning implementation actions. *Cannot be assigned to any approval role on the same CC.* |
| Impact Assessment Owner | SME in the respective area, responsible for identifying the proposed change's potential impact. |
| External Collaborator / Third Party | Complete assigned tasks (impact assessments, change actions) as a non-Lilly user via a temporary external link. |
| Risk Assessment Owner | Assesses known/potential risks and identifies mitigations and/or residual risk. |
| Change Plan Reviewer(s) (Moderate/Major only) | Reviews a proposed change to confirm the content is appropriate. |
| Change Review Board (CRB) (Major only) | Cross-functional/cross-site/multi-site team ensuring consistent, robust review of impact, risk, resources, and timelines for major changes (at minimum); changes with global impact should escalate to a global CRB. CRB verdicts are captured in the record via the Change Plan Reviewer role. |
| Quality Plan Approver(s) | Approves the proposed change before implementation when there's quality impact. Must be a QA representative. |
| HSE Approver(s) | Approves the proposed change before *and* after implementation when there's HSE impact. Must be an HSE representative. |
| Additional Approver(s) | Approves the proposed change before implementation; must include a representative of the impacted functional organization(s) to approve resources/timelines, plus regulatory stakeholders if regulatory impact is identified. |
| QA/Batch Approver (batch-impacting changes only) | Verifies completion of batch-release-impacting change actions. Must be a GMP QA Representative. |
| Change Action Owner | Performs and documents completion of change actions per the change plan. |
| Change Action Reviewer(s) (Moderate/Major only) | Reviews completion of change actions at the end of pre-implementation and implementation phases; may be assigned to any SME, not limited to QA. |
| Quality Implementation Approver(s) | Reviews the entire executed CC to confirm actions meet the pre-approved Change Plan's intent; completes final approval; also approves abandonment if requested. Must be a QA representative. |
| EC Owner | Executes assigned Effectiveness Checks (ECs) per instructions/acceptance criteria; assigns a verdict. |
| EC Verifier | Reviews/approves EC execution and outcomes. Must be Quality (for Quality ECs) or HSE (for HSE ECs). |

### 1.1 Change Control Local Governance

Local sites/areas must apply all requirements from this procedure. Functional sites/areas are responsible for establishing CRB processes, monitoring their own changes, and reporting through Management Review.

### 1.2 Change Control Global Governance

The Change Global Process Owner (GPO) owns the Change Control process, its performance, and the global CRB — managing eQMS changes and global document reviews/updates, monitoring overall system performance, and escalating issues/opportunities to management. HSE retains full governance/ownership of all HSE-Only records, including compliance and timely execution.

## 2.0 Change Pre-Planning

Before initiating a planned GxP change, the Change Owner must refer to GSOP-1405.1-TL.1 (Change Pre-Planning) to determine whether a Change Control is necessary and which system it must be initiated in. Changes from the Supplier Change Notification (SCN) process are managed per GSOP-1301.1-TL.11 (Supplier Change Notification).

## 3.0 Change Initiation

**Responsible Role:** Change Owner

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | Initiate the CC documenting the "What" and "Why": **Change Title**, **Description** (current vs. future state), **Justification** (incl. benefits), **Source** (driver behind the change), **Area(s) of Impact**, and **Due Date** (when all change actions are complete and the implementation summary is approved by the Quality Implementation Approver or, for HSE-only changes, the HSE Approver). |
| 3.2 | Document if applicable: **Emergency Change** (immediate implementation needed to avoid adverse Quality impact, supply interruption affecting patients/end users, or adverse HSE impact — allows implementation before formal Change Plan Approval); **Temporary Change** (time-bound, non-permanent, with both implement and reverse actions — becoming permanent requires a new CC referencing the temporary one); **Parent/Child Change** (hierarchical relationship to other CCs, per GSOP-1405.1-TL.9); **Batch Release Impacting Change** (per GSOP-1405.1-TL.7, if aspects must complete before batch release). |
| 3.3 | If required as an Emergency CC: as soon as possible after verbal agreement, document the emergency description/justification, a preliminary risk assessment, the emergency steps taken and when, and the Quality/Business representatives (minimum M3-equivalent each) who agreed — plus HSE agreement (minimum site HSE leader-equivalent) if there's HSE impact, with that HSE rep included as a "Business" representative during Change Plan approval. Once agreement is obtained, the change may be implemented; those who agreed become Change Plan approvers and remaining steps proceed normally. Otherwise, proceed to 3.4. |
| 3.4 | Document scope details as applicable: related products, materials/batches, assets (e.g., equipment, computer systems), related records (CCs, CAPAs, regulatory events), impacted organizations/regions/third parties, impacted processes/documents, impacted studies, and other impacts. |
| 3.5 | If batch-impacting: associate at least one batch and/or material — this info becomes available to batch disposition personnel once identified, and again once the batch-impacting change actions are completed (see GSOP-1405.1-TL.7). Otherwise, proceed to 4.0. |

## 4.0 Change Classification

A structured, risk-based Change Classification checklist drives classification into **Minor**, **Moderate**, or **Major**, which drives workflow per Appendix A. eQMS has separate Quality and HSE classifications — HSE classification applies when there's health/safety/environmental impact; otherwise Quality classification is required. When both apply, they're determined independently and the higher one drives the workflow (with Quality involvement). See GSOP-1405.1-TL.2 (Quality Change Classification) and GSOP-1405.1-TL.3 (HSE Change Classification) for scoring matrices/criteria.

**Responsible Role:** Change Owner

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Consult Quality and/or HSE as needed and complete the Change Classification checklist: Quality-impact-only → classify per TL.2; HSE-impact-only → classify per TL.3; both → classify per both, with the higher classification driving the workflow. |
| 4.2 | Document rationale for the resulting classification(s), including support for the checklist answers. |

## 5.0 Impact Assessments

**Responsible Role:** Change Owner

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | Impact assessments must consider, at minimum: data integrity, product quality, pharmaceutical quality systems, documentation, validation, regulatory/legal compliance, patient/research subject safety, safety/efficacy information quality & integrity, calibration, maintenance, computer systems, quality control, and other ongoing changes of similar scope. **Moderate/Major** changes: create and assign Impact Assessments to appropriate SME(s) (consider Impact Assessment Templates). **Minor** changes: only an Impact Assessment Summary is required, performed by the Change Owner (independently or on behalf of a group) — then proceed to 7.0. **GMP/Manufacturing area of impact:** refer to GSOP-1405.1-TL.10 to confirm/document whether a CMC Regulatory Review is required, and relate/assign an Impact Assessment to Regulatory if so. **Supplier-performed impact assessments:** assign per GSOP-1301.1-TL.11. |
| 5.2 | Perform impact assessments per GSOP-1405.1-TL.4 (Change Impact Assessment) — the Impact Assessment Owner may work independently or on behalf of a group (SMEs, review panel, cross-functional team). If the Change Description/Justification changes during or after Impact Assessments (before Change Plan approval), the Change Owner must ensure Impact Assessment Owners are informed and can re-perform assessments as needed. |
| 5.3 | Use the AI Impact Assessment Summary Tool to document: how many/what types of impact assessments were completed, and a summary of identified impacts within the scope section. Review/edit the tool's summary for accuracy or to resolve any misalignment between assessments; acknowledge once sufficient. (Change Owner may create this summary manually instead of using the tool.) |
| 5.4 | If a CMC Regulatory Impact Assessment was performed: document the outcome in Regulatory Results; if the verdict is "Do Then Tell" or "Tell/Wait/Do," relate the associated RIM Event to the CC and add the CMC Regulatory representative as an Additional Approver before routing for review/approval. Otherwise, proceed to 6.0. |

## 6.0 Risk Assessment

Identifies/evaluates known and potential risks of a proposed change, focusing on likelihood and severity of adverse effects. **Required** for Moderate/Major CCs; not required beyond the Change Classification checklist's minimal-risk determination for Minor CCs.

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Create and assign Risk Assessments based on CC details, scope, classification, and identified impacts. See GSOP-1405.1-TL.5 for required formality level. A relation to a pre-existing risk evaluation may substitute for a new one if it appropriately covers the CC's risk. | Change Owner |
| 6.2 | Perform Risk Assessments per GSOP-1405.1-TL.5. | Risk Assessment Owner |

## 7.0 Change Plan Definition

**Responsible Role:** Change Owner

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 7.1 | Once impact/risk assessments are complete, review the classification to confirm it still accurately reflects the CC's severity/complexity. |
| 7.2 | Document the change plan: objectives/milestones/timelines and dependencies; a high-level phased-implementation summary with risk-mitigation actions; the Batch Impacting Change actions strategy per GSOP-1405.1-TL.7 (if applicable); scope/systems involved; and functions/resources needed. |
| 7.3 | Create and assign Change Actions to Pre-Implementation, Implementation, and/or Post-Implementation phases. Each action must describe what will be performed, specify success criteria, and be flagged batch-impacting per GSOP-1405.1-TL.7 if applicable. Batch-impacting CCs need at least one batch-impacting action; temporary CCs need both implement and reverse actions. Consider change action templates (see GSOP-1405.1-RS.1, Change Action Phasing); assign supplier actions per GSOP-1301.1-TL.11. *All CCs need at least one action (any phase); using all three phases isn't mandatory. The "Other" phase is for the Change Update process. Action due dates are targets, not hard requirements.* |
| 7.4 | **Moderate/Major:** create an EC and document an EC plan per GSOP-1405.1-TL.11 to verify the CC met its goals, detect issues/unintended consequences (realized residual risk, adverse impacts, related deviations), and confirm sustainability over the EC's monitoring period. (A parent CC's EC may cover child CCs if effectiveness measurement mutually applies.) **Minor:** the Implementation Summary itself serves as the effectiveness evaluation — proceed to 7.5. |
| 7.5 | Route the CC for review/approval — minimum: a Quality and/or HSE approver (per classification area; not required if that area has no impact) and an Additional Approver from the impacted site/function(s) for resources/timelines. Additional approvers (incl. Regulatory) may be added if identified during impact assessment. |

**Minimum reviewer/approver levels by classification** (table extraction was inconsistent in the source PDF — see the PDF/eQMS directly for the exact matrix): Minor requires no Change Plan Reviewer/CRB; Moderate requires a Change Plan Reviewer; Major requires SME(s) and a CRB representative. Approvers across all levels include a Quality Approver (Quality Unit Representative), HSE Approver (HSE Unit Representative), and Additional Approver(s) (Business/Organization Unit Representative, M2/P4/R4 or higher, with M3/P5/R6 or higher for higher classifications).

## 8.0 Change Plan Review and Approval

| Step | Condition | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Moderate Change | Confirm: description/justification/classification properly documented; scope is accurate and sufficiently detailed (e.g., batch numbers); impacts/risks accurately assessed and mitigated in the change plan; change actions adequately defined (incl. batch-impacting designation); EC criteria are appropriate/measurable. Otherwise proceed to 8.2. | Change Control Reviewer (Moderate) |
| 8.2 | Major Change | Major changes must be presented to and approved by the CRB (documented by a CRB member in the Review step). CRB confirms the same criteria listed in 8.1. | Change Review Board (CRB) |
| 8.3 | — | Approval confirms the record was reviewed for accuracy/completeness (per the approver's role) and that risk(s) are appropriately identified/mitigated and the change is authorized for implementation. *Any Change Plan modification must be re-approved by all original approvers or equivalent; implementation-plan/success-criteria modifications require appropriate quality oversight.* | Quality Plan Approver / Additional Approver / HSE Approver |

## 9.0 Change Implementation

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | Perform and document evidence of actions per their completion criteria. If execution varies but the action's intent is still met, document the variation. If the intent can't be met, contact the Change Owner and Quality/HSE to discuss and document justification before progressing. | Change Action Owner |
| 9.2 | For Moderate/Major changes in Pre-Implementation or Implementation phases: review all executed actions in the phase for evidence of completion per criteria (or sufficient rationale for how intent was met); if not, return with comments for edits. *Actions in a prior phase must be reviewed before the next phase's actions can begin* (see GSOP-1405.1-RS.1). Otherwise proceed to 9.3. | Change Action Reviewer |
| 9.3 | If the action is batch-impacting, verify it per GSOP-1405.1-TL.7. Otherwise proceed to 9.4. | QA Batch Approver |
| 9.4 | If an action is overdue, modify its due date to the realistic completion date. If that pushes past the CC's own due date, an Extension Request is required (contact the Change Owner to perform one per GSOP-1405.1-TL.6). Otherwise proceed to 9.5. | Change Action Owner |
| 9.5 | If the CC's scope has changed, or actions need adding/updating, use Change Updates to correct/clarify scope or revise action extent for resource changes (not to modify the Change Plan's *intent* — that requires abandonment + a new CC). Review/update the Change Plan, add/update incomplete actions with rationale, describe the update holistically with justification, and route back to the original (or equivalent) Change Plan Approvers for the same level of approval as initially required. *Change Actions cannot progress once a Change Update is initiated, until Update Approval; once a phase starts/completes, actions can't be added to it — use "Other" actions instead.* Otherwise proceed to 9.6. | Change Owner |
| 9.6 | If the CC can/should no longer be implemented, or changes would significantly alter the original impact/risk assessment: abandon it — use Change Update to create/perform actions undoing completed ones that impact the original state, and cancel outstanding actions. Document rationale and route for abandonment approval. *Abandonment is only for use before all actions complete — not for failed implementations.* Otherwise proceed to 9.7. | Change Owner |
| 9.7 | Once implementation is complete, use the AI Implementation Summary Tool to generate a summary: whether implementation progressed as planned; whether actions matched their description/completion criteria (with justification for any departure); and a holistic statement on whether all actions met the Change Plan's intent (with justification for any departure). Review/edit the summary, noting next steps (e.g., new CC, deviation) if misaligned. Record the actual implementation date. Route for approval. (Change Owner may create this summary manually instead of using the tool.) | Change Owner |
| 9.8 | Review/approve if the implementation summary is accurate and completed actions/summary meet the change plan's objectives. If summary documentation is insufficient, reject to the Change Owner for corrections. If action documentation is insufficient, return to implementation for more detail. If implementation didn't match the Change Plan's intent, ensure appropriate next steps (new CC, deviation, etc.) before approving. | Quality Implementation Approver / HSE Approver |

## 10.0 Effectiveness Check

For Moderate/Major changes, perform effectiveness checks per GSOP-1405.1-TL.11.

## 11.0 Extension Requests

Overdue CCs must be extended before routing for final approval. The Change Owner initiates an extension as soon as it's known the CC won't finish by its due date, per GSOP-1405.1-TL.6.

## 12.0 Amendment

Amendments only add supporting information to a *closed* CC — they must not affect original scope/intent.

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 12.1 | Contact QA with rationale to initiate the Amendment process, regardless of classification. | Change Owner |
| 12.2 | Confirm the proposed amendment has sufficient rationale and won't alter original scope/intent; if so, initiate the amendment workflow. | Quality Implementation Approver |
| 12.3 | Add the amended information and route to the original (or equivalent) Change Plan Approvers. | Change Owner |
| 12.4 | Evaluate whether the amendment altered original scope/intent: **Acceptable** → approve (closes the CC record); **Not Acceptable** → reject with rationale, returned to the amendment owner for revision. | Quality Implementation Approver |

## Appendix A: Workflow Driven by Change Classification

Classification level determines workflow depth/structure so control is commensurate with risk/complexity — Minor changes are streamlined (fewer risk-mitigation workflow elements, faster/simpler execution); Moderate and Major changes share a similar structure (formal evaluations, impact/risk assessments, effectiveness checks) that varies only in depth/formality.

| Workflow Element | Minor Change | Moderate/Major Change |
|---|---|---|
| Impact Assessment (IA) | Required (IA Summary only — IA task(s) not required) | Required (both IA task(s) and IA Summary) |
| Risk Assessment (RA) | Not required | Required (General or FMEA) |
| Child Changes | Not allowed | Allowed |
| Change Plan Review | Not allowed | Allowed |
| Post-Phase Review of Change Action | Not allowed | Required |
| Effectiveness Check (EC) Creation | Not allowed | Required |
