**Update about new usage tiers in Claude — here's how to stay well inside yours**

We've rolled out monthly budget tiers for Claude access at Lilly. Most of you will never notice them, we set monthly limits at the 95th percentile based on ~30,000 people. The rest of this post covers how the tiers work, how to check where you stand, and a handful of habits that keep you comfortably inside your budget.

**The tiers at a glance: Tier | Monthly Budget | Who/how Default (Chat) | $250 | Everyone!**

**Medium Usage| $500 | Approved upgrade you can request** [**here.**](https://myaccess.microsoft.com/@lilly.com#/access-packages/999d1d5e-a5ec-49c9-ad48-bd2f5aeaf650 "https://myaccess.microsoft.com/@lilly.com#/access-packages/999d1d5e-a5ec-49c9-ad48-bd2f5aeaf650")

**Check your spend anytime:** [**https://claude.is.lilly.com/analytics/me**](https://claude.is.lilly.com/analytics/me "https://claude.is.lilly.com/analytics/me") **Eight habits that keep your budget healthy:**

  

- **New topic → new chat. We now pay per usage, where usage is measured in chat length.** Longer chats with unrelated content cost more and give worse answers than small, focused conversations. **[BIG]**
- **Pick the right model.** Haiku for quick lookups, Sonnet for daily work (3X Haiku), Opus for heavy reasoning (5X Haiku). Don't default to Opus for easy one-liners or light formatting. Experiment with using smaller models and 'upgrade' as needed. **[BIG]**
- **Use Projects.** Upload your docs once and ask many questions against them — don't re-attach files every new chat. **[BIG]**
- **Avoid “cold” chats:** While you're actively chatting, Claude does some magic to save recent messages. After ~5 minutes of idle time that disappears, the next message re-bills the whole conversation at full rate. Coming back tomorrow → start fresh and paste in only what you still need. **[BIG]**
- **Turn off web search and other tools** you aren't using. If there are tools you never need (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov), turn them off from Customize -> Connectors. **[MEDIUM]**
- **Turn off extended thinking** when you don't need it. **[SMALL]**
- **Batch related questions** in one chat instead of starting a new one for each. **[SMALL]**
- **Watch your own usage** — a 30-second check once a week beats a surprise at month end. Thanks for your patience as we continue to scale Claude across Lilly. We're committed to making this platform work for everyone, and these tiers help us keep Claude accessible and sustainable as adoption grows.
