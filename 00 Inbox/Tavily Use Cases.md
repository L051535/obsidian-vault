**1. Regulatory & Compliance Monitoring**  
Pharma companies must track evolving laws, FDA/EMA guidelines, and global health regulations. Tavily can monitor official government and regulator sites, summarizing new rulings, enforcement actions, and policy updates with citations, enabling teams to act quickly on compliance changes.

**2. Competitive & Market Intelligence**  
Tavily can track competitor product launches, pricing changes, and market positioning by pulling live reports, press releases, and industry benchmarks. This helps pharma teams spot market shifts early and adjust strategies accordingly.

**3. Vendor & Supply Chain Risk**  
Pharma supply chains are complex and vulnerable to disruptions. Tavily can monitor vendor news, sanctions lists, and supply chain disruptions in real time, enabling proactive risk mitigation.
