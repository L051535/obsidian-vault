**Current Meeting Summary**

**1. Core Theme**

The meeting focused on **supply chain risk management**, with heavy emphasis on **theft, counterfeiting, geopolitical disruption, and logistics resilience**, particularly for **high‑value pharmaceutical products**.

**2. Theft, Counterfeiting & Security Risks**

- **Distribution and last‑mile logistics** were repeatedly identified as the **highest‑risk points** for theft.
- Tablet products pose elevated risk due to **portability and black‑market value**.
- **Hybrid distribution models** (where Lilly retains custody longer) increase exposure, especially outside the US.
- A key gap was confirmed:

- Lilly has defined security standards for **internal sites and warehouses**
- **CMO production, staging, and testing sites lack formal security requirements**

- Actions underway:

- Preliminary **CMO security assessments**
- Evaluation of **right‑sized controls** based on theft likelihood and supply‑chain step

**3. Distribution Models & Benchmarking**

- UK example highlighted:

- Theft occurring primarily at **logistics service providers (LSPs)**, not wholesalers
- High geographic dispersion of depots increases risk
- Consolidation of depots and **stronger security provisions** proposed

- Consensus to benchmark with:

- **Big three US wholesalers**
- **PCSC / HDA security leadership**

- Objective:

- Understand what theft levels are considered **“typical loss”**
- Align on **baseline vs. enhanced security expectations** for high‑value products

**4. Technology & Anti‑Counterfeiting**

- **RFID** discussed as a potential enabler for:

- Theft detection
- Tracking
- Product authentication

- Confirmed:

- Studies exist but remain **confidential**
- RFID is **under evaluation**, not yet deployed broadly
- Legal and counterfeiting teams are exploring **alternatives to 2D labels**

**5. Operational Events – Last Six Months**

- Review completed of recent operational disruptions:

- Middle East conflict (largest and most sustained impact)
- Weather events (US hurricanes and winter storms)
- Transportation incidents
- Government shutdown

- Key takeaway:

- Most disruptions originate in **logistics**, with downstream impact to production and supply.

**6. Middle East Conflict – Transportation Impact**

- Event required **rapid weekend mobilization** and daily coordination.
- Main challenges:

- Limited real‑time visibility of **in‑transit shipments**
- Quality approvals needed for non‑standard routing

- Primary mitigation approach:

- **Consolidation via Saudi Arabia (Riyadh)** with redistribution to the region
- Opportunistic use of limited airport openings

- **Lebanon identified as the most difficult market** to supply.
- Success drivers:

- Close coordination with affiliates, freight forwarders, and **local governments**
- Planning teams kept **production running or accelerated** to be ready when lanes reopened

- Key learning:

- Speed, relationships, and influence determine access to constrained capacity.

**7. Inventory Strategy & Business Alignment**

- Conflict exposed tension between:

- **Operational inventory targets**
- **Strategic buffers for geopolitical risk**

- Ongoing discussion to:

- Align with business units on **higher strategic inventory levels** in high‑risk regions
- Balance resilience against cost

**8. Energy Risk in Asia**

- Assessment highlighted **energy‑driven supply risk**, not just cost increases.
- Key risks:

- **Naphtha feedstock shortages** impacting Asian chemical production
- **Japan** facing LNG constraints and emerging industrial blackouts
- **China (Jiangsu province)** has precedent for forced industrial shutdowns

- Specific supplier exposure identified for **Orforglipron inputs**.
- Recommended follow‑ups:

- Confirm supplier inventory and production continuity
- Review Lilly buffer stocks and alternate sourcing readiness

**9. Governance, Monitoring & Resilience**

- Case study (North Carolina winter storm):

- Site heads and network leaders currently retain **final decision authority** in crises
- Governance model under review

- Gap acknowledged:

- No 24/7 internal monitoring capability

- Proposal under leadership review to expand monitoring using **India support resources**.
