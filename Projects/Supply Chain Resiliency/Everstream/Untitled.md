## 🧭 What’s the focus

- Align on how **Everstream can support Lilly’s supply chain risk visibility**, especially across **logistics, planning, and procurement**
- Evaluate how Everstream fits alongside **Project44 (P44), SAP TM, Kinaxis**, and broader digital ecosystem initiatives
- Identify **practical use cases, ownership, and next steps** for deploying insights across teams

## ⚙️ Key discussion themes

### 1) Everstream vs. Project44 (P44)

- **P44 = real-time shipment visibility (“dot on map”)**
- **Everstream = external risk intelligence (weather, strikes, cyber, geopolitical, etc.)**
- Current integration between Everstream and P44:
    - Exists but **not fully optimized**
    - Limited data sharing and signal quality
- Consensus:
    - Tools are **complementary, not duplicative**
    - Near-term approach likely:
        - Use Everstream **standalone**
        - Integrate primarily via **SAP TM / TMS layer**

### 2) Core use cases for Everstream

**A. Network-level risk visibility**

- Monitoring:
    - Suppliers, ports, DCs, logistics providers
    - Global risks (cyber attacks, weather, strikes)
- Example: Identifying ripple effects from large disruptions (e.g., geopolitical crises)

**B. Pre-transit planning (high value)**

- Planners can:
    - Identify risks **before shipment leaves**
    - Adjust routes/ports proactively
- Seen as one of the **most valuable use cases**

**C. In-transit awareness**

- Alerts triggered after route planning:
    - Issues flagged in SAP TM shortly after planning
- Helps decision-making, but **not fully proactive**

**D. Medium-term planning (1–4 weeks out)**

- Use **incident start/end predictions** to:
    - Adjust production plans (Kinaxis)
    - Reallocate inventory or capacity

### 3) Big gap: Nodes vs. lanes

- Current Lilly setup:
    - ✅ Strong on **nodes** (sites, suppliers, DCs)
    - ❌ Missing **lane visibility** (connections between nodes)
- Impact:
    - Limits ability to:
        - Predict route risks
        - Connect disruptions to shipments
- Key takeaway: → **Adding lanes (or even “likely routes”) is a critical next step**

### 4) Visibility gaps in inbound logistics

- Limited insight into:
    - Supplier → site movements (inbound)
- Identified opportunities:
    - Leverage **freight forwarder integrations (e.g., DHL via P44)**
    - Expand scope of **global logistics ownership upstream**
- Strong linkage to:
    - Yard management
    - Dock scheduling
    - Predictive ETAs

### 5) Cross-functional coordination challenge

- Current state:
    - Teams operate in **silos** (planning, logistics, procurement)
- Need:
    - A **shared process / “risk ecosystem”**
    - Better flow of insight:
        - From planners → logistics → customer service
- Example gap:
    - Disruption identified upstream does not always reach execution teams

### 6) Emerging operating model ideas

**A. Risk “hub” / council**

- Monthly alignment + daily cadence during crisis
- Central team curates alerts and distributes insights

**B. Daily triage workflows**

- Review:
    - Last 24h incidents
    - Upcoming risks
- Prioritize “top risks” for action

**C. Role-based views & alerts**

- Tailored dashboards for:
    - Logistics
    - Planning
    - Executives (digest emails)

### 7) Advanced capabilities discussed

- **Connected incidents (ripple effects)**  
    → e.g., Iran conflict → airline changes → capacity impacts globally
    
- **Weather intelligence (strong differentiator)**  
    → Highly precise, facility-level forecasting  
    → Valuable for proactive planning
    
- **External risk monitoring beyond shipments** → e.g., cyber attacks on logistics providers (major concern at Lilly)
    

### 8) Practical friction points raised

- Manual effort to:
    - Add locations (ports, warehouses, logistics partners)
    - Maintain facility lists and groupings
- Limited:
    - Audit trail / ownership tracking in triage workflow
- Open questions on:
    - Ownership of decisions
    - How insights flow into execution systems (P44, SAP, Kinaxis)

## ⚠️ Key risks / tensions

- Integration complexity with P44 (current limitations)
- Organizational alignment:
    - Who owns what decisions?
    - How much access to give site teams
- Risk of **too much noise vs. actionable signals**
- Gap between **insight generation and operational action**

## ✅ What’s landing clearly

- Everstream provides **unique value at the network + risk intelligence layer**
- Biggest unlock = **connecting data + process across functions**
- Immediate opportunity: → Improve **lane visibility + inbound tracking + workflow ownership**

## ➡️ Likely next steps (implied)

- Define:
    - **Priority use cases** (logistics vs planning vs network)
- Close data gaps:
    - Add **lanes / route approximations**
- Clarify operating model:
    - Who triages, who acts, how it flows
- Evaluate integration strategy:
    - Everstream ↔ SAP TM ↔ P44 ↔ Kinaxis
