## Decisions & Context

> [!info]- Background
> Core finding: no evidence of historical theft, and controls are strong for bulk/production/transport stages — but printed packaging materials at external suppliers were flagged as a critical, largely unassessed gap, and risk rises sharply once product is broken down to unit/bottle level in Lilly Direct-style distribution. Leadership asked the team to focus on likelihood classification first, mitigations second.
