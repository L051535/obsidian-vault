**Purpose**

- Review end‑to‑end **Orfo theft risk** across **US distribution**, using a node‑and‑flow risk model.
- Align on **prioritization of high‑risk points**, current mitigations, and next steps.
- Set sequencing for **UK, EU, UAE, and other markets**.

---

## Key Framework & Methodology

- Risk assessed across **nodes (facilities, handoff points)** and **flows (transport, last mile)**.
- Three scoring dimensions:
    - **Likelihood** – assessed assuming _no mitigations_ in place (relative prioritization).
    - **Impact** - impact if theft were to occur, based on volume and pack form
    - **Detectability** – ability to detect and trace theft after it occurs (mostly reactive).
    - **Security Coverage** – proactive controls currently in place (e.g., GPS, access controls).
- **Custody visibility**:
    - **Red** = Lilly maintains custody
    - **Gray** = Lilly does not maintain custody
- Trucking **lanes were not individually assessed**; instead, **qualified transportation providers** were evaluated.

---

## Major Discussion Points & Findings

### Manufacturing & CMOs

- **Internal Lilly sites**: Generally considered **low risk** due to established security controls and validated transportation lanes.
- **External CMOs**:
    - **PCI** identified as the **highest‑priority risk** due to volume concentration for US launch.
    - **Pfizer Ascoli** prioritized next.
    - Several CMOs lack completed **security coverage assessments** → ongoing EM team evaluations.
    - Readouts expected soon from EM teams (led by Jean O’Driscoll / Alayna Sherman).

### Warehousing & 3PLs

- **External warehouses (e.g., NALO, Langham, LSL)** flagged as **high‑impact risk areas**.
- Concerns raised:
    - Open facilities / parking
    - Limited access control or receptionist presence
    - Contract labor and employee screening practices
- Recommendation:
    - **Deeper, on‑site assessments** (not desk audits)
    - Increase **priority for NALO and shared warehouses**
    - Consider **group / concentrated audits** across facilities for consistency

### Transportation

- **US trucking providers** rated **medium likelihood / medium impact**:
    - Continuous qualification via policy and **TAPA standards**
    - **Live GPS tracking required** (currently GPS only; no satellite vs cellular distinction)
- Discussion noted vulnerability to **cellular jamming** (e.g., Colombia case), but standard remains GPS-based.

### Printed Packaging Materials

- Identified as a **critical gap**:
    - Security and inventory visibility at **packaging material suppliers** (used by Lilly and CMOs) is limited.
    - Global Procurement is leading parallel assessments.
    - Considered **very high priority** due to counterfeit and diversion risk.

### Lilly Direct & Last‑Mile Risk

- Last‑mile delivery identified as a **theft risk**, especially based on UK experience.
- **No signature required** currently for Lilly Direct shipments (confirmed).
- Open questions:
    - Reassess **signature‑required delivery** given Orfo launch (cost vs. risk trade‑off).
    - Use of **patient-selected pickup points** (e.g., UPS Access Point) vs. home delivery.
- Packaging concerns:
    - Planned **“pretty” / branded box** for Lilly Direct may increase theft risk.
    - Discussion about learning from other platforms that use **blinded packaging** for both privacy and security.

---

## Geographic Prioritization

1. **United States** – immediate focus
2. **UAE** – upcoming launch, assumed lower theft risk but still to be reviewed
3. **UK** – high priority after US due to known theft incidence
4. **EU (Germany, France first; Spain/Italy later)**
5. **China** – low likelihood but potentially high impact due to future volume

---

## Agreed Next Steps

- Continue and accelerate:
    - **CMO security assessments** (PCI first, then Pfizer Ascoli)
    - **Deeper facility audits** for high‑risk warehouses (NALO, LSL, shared 3PLs)
- Elevate **printed packaging supplier security** review with Procurement.
- Engage **Lilly Direct / Commercial** team to:
    - Decide on **signature‑required delivery**
    - Reassess **branding vs. blinding** of shipment packaging
    - Document accepted risks if mitigation is not adopted
- Brooke to provide **status updates via email** and convene targeted follow‑ups as decisions emerge.
